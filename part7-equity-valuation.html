1. 相对估值：P/E 乘数 Relative Valuation

Chapter 3.2 · slides 3–9Practice Q15(a)(b)(c)
▸ Definition

Relative valuation values an asset by comparing it with the market prices of comparable assets, using a standardised multiple such as the price-earnings ratio. Trailing P/E uses realised (last twelve months) EPS; forward P/E uses expected EPS.

P/E \[ \text{Trailing P/E}=\frac{P_0}{EPS_{trailing}} \qquad \text{Forward P/E}=\frac{P_0}{EPS_{forward}} \]
当预期 EPS 高于已实现 EPS 时，分母变大 → Forward P/E 低于 Trailing P/E（价格固定）。
▸ 两个必考的陷阱
  1. 乘数必须与匹配的盈利口径相乘：用可比公司的 trailing P/E，就要乘目标公司的 trailing EPS；混用会得出错误的估值。
  2. 相对便宜 ≠ 绝对便宜：如果整个可比组都被高估，那么「组内最便宜」的股票仍然可能是贵的。相对估值只能回答「相对于同伴」，不能回答「相对于内在价值」。
EXAMPLE 1 Trailing vs forward P/E and peer-based value
EN

Firm C has trailing EPS of $3.00 and expected EPS next year of $3.60. Its share price is $54. Comparable firms have an average trailing P/E of 20.
(a) Calculate Firm C's trailing P/E and forward P/E. Explain why they differ.
(b) Estimate its value using the peer trailing P/E and the matching EPS measure. Compare your estimate with the market price.
(c) Give two economic reasons why Firm C might deserve a lower P/E than its peers. Why does a low relative valuation not prove that the share is cheap in absolute terms?

中文分析

(a) 两个 P/E 的差别全部来自分母：价格固定为 \$54，预期 EPS（\$3.60）高于已实现 EPS（\$3.00），所以 forward P/E 更低。
(b) 可比公司给的是 trailing P/E，所以必须乘 trailing EPS（\$3.00），得到 \$60；与市价 \$54 相比低 \$6。
(c) P/E 低通常反映增长预期更低或风险更高；而且相对便宜不代表绝对便宜。

公式 \[ \text{Value}=EPS_{trailing}\times(\text{peer trailing P/E}) \]
解题步骤
  1. (a) Trailing P/E \(=54/3.00=\mathbf{18.0}\)。
  2. (a) Forward P/E \(=54/3.60=\mathbf{15.0}\)。
  3. (a) 差异原因：预期盈利高于已实现盈利，价格不变时分母变大 → forward 比率更低。
  4. (b) 估值 \(=3.00\times20=\mathbf{\$60}\)。
  5. (b) 与市价比较：\(\$60-\$54=\$6\)，市价比估计值低 \$6。
  6. (c) 合理更低 P/E 的两个理由：预期增长更低、经营或财务风险更高（盈利质量差异也可作为第三点）。
  7. (c) 相对便宜 ≠ 绝对便宜：整个可比组都可能被高估，因此相对估值不能证明绝对价值。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) Trailing P/E = 54/3 = 18; forward P/E = 54/3.60 = 15. The forward ratio is lower because expected earnings exceed trailing earnings with price held fixed.
(b) Peer-based value = trailing EPS × peer trailing P/E = 3 × 20 = \$60; the market price is \$6 below this estimate.
(c) Lower expected growth and greater business or financial risk are two valid reasons (earnings quality may also matter). The whole peer group may be overpriced, so relative cheapness does not establish absolute value.

中文答案

(a) Trailing 18、Forward 15（预期盈利更高 → 分母更大 → 比率更低）；(b) 估计值 \$60，市价低 \$6；(c) 增长更低、风险更高；且整组可能都被高估，相对便宜不等于绝对便宜。
易错点：用 peer trailing P/E 乘 forward EPS（= \$72）是典型错误，口径必须匹配。

2. 股利折现模型 Dividend Discount Model

Chapter 3.2 · slides 10–25Practice Q14
▸ Definition

Dividend discount model (DDM): the value of a share equals the present value of all expected future dividends, discounted at the required return on equity.

零增长（永续年金） \[ P_0=\frac{D}{r} \]
适用于股利恒定不变的情形，典型例子是优先股 preferred share。
固定增长（戈登增长模型） \[ P_0=\frac{D_1}{r-g}=\frac{D_0(1+g)}{r-g},\qquad r>g \]
\(D_1\) = 下一期股利；\(r\) = 要求回报率；\(g\) = 永续增长率。必须用 \(D_1\) 而不是刚派发的 \(D_0\)，且必须 \(r>g\)，否则级数不收敛、公式无意义。
非常规增长（分段 + 终值） \[ P_0=\sum_{t=1}^{T}\frac{D_t}{(1+r)^t}+\frac{TV_T}{(1+r)^T},\qquad TV_T=\frac{D_{T+1}}{r-g}=\frac{D_T(1+g)}{r-g} \]
终值 \(TV_T\) 位于第 \(T\) 期末（即刚付完 \(D_T\) 之后），代表此后所有股利的现值，需要再折现 \(T\) 期回到今天。
▸ 三个必须写进答案的条件
  1. 用 \(D_1\)：公式里的分子是下一期股利，题目通常给的是「刚派发的 \(D_0\)」，要乘 \((1+g)\) 转换。
  2. \(r>g\)：否则无穷级数不收敛，模型给出无限大或负值，没有意义。
  3. 反解回报：\(r=D_1/P_0+g\)，即「股利收益率 + 增长率」，可与要求回报率比较来判断高估/低估。
EXAMPLE 2 Constant-growth DDM, nonconstant growth and preferred shares
EN

Firm A has just paid an annual dividend of $2.00 per share. Dividends are expected to grow at 4% per year indefinitely. The required annual return is 9%.
(a) Calculate next year's dividend and the current value of Firm A's shares under the constant-growth DDM.
(b) If the market price is $40, calculate the expected return under the same dividend-growth assumptions. Compare it with the required return.
(c) Explain why the constant-growth formula requires the required return to exceed the perpetual growth rate.
(d) Firm B will pay $2.00 in Year 1 and $2.40 in Year 2. After Year 2, dividends grow at 3% forever. With a 9% required return, calculate the terminal value at the end of Year 2 and the current share value.
(e) A preferred share pays a constant annual dividend of $3 forever, starting one year from now. Calculate its value at a required return of 6%.

中文分析

(a) 题目给的是「刚派发」的 \(D_0=2.00\)，必须先算出 \(D_1=2.00\times1.04=2.08\) 再代入。
(b) 反解公式 \(r=D_1/P_0+g\)：把市价当作 \(P_0\) 代进去，得到的就是「按这个价格买入的预期回报」。
(d) 终值在第 2 期末（刚付完 \(D_2\) 后），分子是 \(D_3=D_2(1+g)\)；然后 \(D_1\)、\(D_2+TV_2\) 分别折现。

公式 \[ D_1=D_0(1+g) \qquad P_0=\frac{D_1}{r-g} \qquad r=\frac{D_1}{P_0}+g \qquad TV_2=\frac{D_3}{r-g} \]
解题步骤
  1. (a) \(D_1=2.00\times1.04=\mathbf{\$2.08}\)。
  2. (a) \(P_0=2.08/(0.09-0.04)=2.08/0.05=\mathbf{\$41.60}\)。
  3. (b) \(r=2.08/40+0.04=0.052+0.04=\mathbf{9.20\%}\)。
  4. (b) 与要求回报率比较：\(9.20\%>9.00\%\)，高出 0.20 个百分点 → 在该假设下市价 \$40 略偏低（预期回报高于要求回报）。
  5. (c) 原因：贴现股利级数只有在 \(r>g\) 时收敛；若 \(g\ge r\) 永续成立，无穷和不产生有限值。
  6. (d) \(D_3=2.40\times1.03=\$2.472\)；\(TV_2=2.472/(0.09-0.03)=2.472/0.06=\mathbf{\$41.20}\)。
  7. (d) 现值 \(=\dfrac{2.00}{1.09}+\dfrac{2.40+41.20}{1.09^2}=1.8349+36.6900=\mathbf{\$38.53}\)。
  8. (e) 优先股（零增长永续）：\(P=3/0.06=\mathbf{\$50.00}\)。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) \(D_1=2.00\times1.04=\mathbf{\$2.08}\); \(P_0=2.08/(0.09-0.04)=\mathbf{\$41.60}\).
(b) Expected return \(=2.08/40+0.04=\mathbf{9.20\%}\), exceeding the required 9% by 0.20 pp.
(c) The discounted dividend series converges only when \(r>g\); if \(g\ge r\) indefinitely the infinite sum has no finite value.
(d) \(D_3=2.40\times1.03=2.472\); \(TV_2=2.472/(0.09-0.03)=\mathbf{\$41.20}\); \(P_0=2/1.09+(2.40+41.20)/1.09^2=\mathbf{\$38.53}\).
(e) \(P=3/0.06=\mathbf{\$50.00}\).

中文答案

(a) \(D_1=\)\$2.08，价值 \$41.60；(b) 预期回报 9.20%，高于要求回报 0.20 pp；(c) \(r>g\) 是级数收敛的必要条件；
(d) 终值 \$41.20，当前价值 \$38.53；(e) 优先股 \$50.00。
易错点汇总：①把 \(D_0\) 当 \(D_1\) 直接代入；②(d) 中终值分子用 \(D_2\) 而非 \(D_3\)；③终值忘记再折现 2 期。

3. CAPM：确定折现率 Capital Asset Pricing Model

Chapter 3.2 · slides 26–32Practice Q15(d)(e)
▸ Definition

CAPM: in equilibrium, the expected return on an asset equals the risk-free rate plus a premium proportional to its beta — its exposure to market (systematic) risk.

CAPM \[ E(r_i)=r_f+\beta_i\big[E(r_M)-r_f\big] \qquad \beta_i=\frac{\mathrm{Cov}(r_i,r_M)}{\sigma_M^2} \]
\(r_f\) = 无风险利率；\(E(r_M)-r_f\) = 市场风险溢价 market risk premium；\(\beta_i\) = 该资产对市场组合的敏感度。
▸ 为什么 CAPM 只给系统性风险溢价？

投资者可以通过分散化消除公司特有风险（firm-specific / idiosyncratic risk），因此持有这种风险不会得到额外补偿。对组合风险真正有贡献的是该股票对市场波动的暴露程度，即 beta。所以不是所有的单独波动率都能换来溢价。
答题句式：In CAPM, diversified investors can reduce firm-specific risk through diversification; the relevant contribution to portfolio risk is the stock's exposure to market movements, measured by beta.

EXAMPLE 3 CAPM required return
EN

The risk-free rate is 3%, the expected market return is 8%, and Firm C's beta is 1.2.
(a) Calculate its CAPM required return.
(b) Explain why CAPM rewards exposure to market risk rather than every source of a stock's stand-alone volatility.
(c) If the market risk premium doubles while beta and the risk-free rate stay the same, what is the new required return?

中文分析

(a) 直接代入：市场风险溢价 = 8% − 3% = 5%，乘以 beta 1.2 得 6%，加上无风险利率 3% = 9%。
(c) 市场风险溢价翻倍 → 5% × 2 = 10%；新要求回报 = 3% + 1.2 × 10% = 15%。注意是溢价翻倍，不是总回报翻倍。

公式 \[ E(r_i)=3\%+1.2\,(8\%-3\%)=3\%+1.2\times5\% \]
解题步骤
  1. (a) 市场风险溢价 \(=8\%-3\%=5\%\)。
  2. (a) 风险溢价部分 \(=1.2\times5\%=6\%\)。
  3. (a) 要求回报 \(=3\%+6\%=\mathbf{9.00\%}\)。
  4. (b) 解释：分散化消除个体风险 → 只有不可分散的市场暴露（beta）获得溢价。
  5. (c) 新溢价 \(=10\%\) → 新要求回报 \(=3\%+1.2\times10\%=\mathbf{15.00\%}\)。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) \(E(r)=3\%+1.2(8\%-3\%)=\mathbf{9.00\%}\).
(b) Diversified investors can eliminate firm-specific risk, so only beta — exposure to market movements — is priced; not all stand-alone volatility earns a premium.
(c) New premium = 10%; new required return = \(3\%+1.2(10\%)=\mathbf{15.00\%}\).

中文答案

(a) 9.00%；(b) 只有系统性风险（beta）获溢价，个体风险可通过分散化消除；(c) 15.00%。
提醒：(c) 中是市场风险溢价翻倍（5% → 10%），不是要求回报翻倍。

4. Fama-French 三因子与动量 Factor Models

Chapter 3.2 · slides 27–42Practice Q16
▸ Definition

Fama-French three-factor model extends CAPM by adding two portfolio return series that capture size and value effects: SMB (small minus big) and HML (high minus low book-to-market).

三因子模型 \[ r_i-r_f=\alpha+b_M\,\text{MKTRF}+b_S\,\text{SMB}+b_H\,\text{HML}+\varepsilon \]
MKTRF = 市场收益 − 无风险利率（market excess return）；
SMB = Small minus Big：小市值组合减大市值组合的收益；
HML = High minus Low：高账面市值比（价值股）减低账面市值比（成长股）组合的收益；
MOM（动量） = 近期赢家减近期输家的收益（课件提及）。
▸ 载荷符号怎么读
  • SMB 载荷为正 → 该组合偏向小盘股；
  • HML 载荷为正 → 偏向价值股（高账面市值比）；为负 → 偏向成长股。
  • 因子收益与载荷同号时贡献为正：例如 HML 收益为 −0.5%、载荷为 −0.2，乘积为 +0.10%。
▸ 必考场景：CAPM 有正 alpha，三因子下 alpha ≈ 0

这说明此前的「超额收益」并非管理能力，而是该基金承担了规模与价值因子的暴露——CAPM 因为遗漏这些因子，把因子收益错误地记入了截距项 alpha。加入因子后，可解释的部分被吸收，截距自然趋近于 0。
严谨表述：Alpha near zero is consistent with factor exposure accounting for the earlier alpha; without further evidence it does not establish statistical significance or prove that skill is absent.

EXAMPLE 4 Interpreting a three-factor regression
EN

Monthly Fama-French three-factor regression: excess fund return \(=\alpha+b_M\cdot\text{MKTRF}+b_S\cdot\text{SMB}+b_H\cdot\text{HML}+\) residual. Loadings: \(b_M=1.1\), \(b_S=0.5\), \(b_H=-0.2\).
(a) Explain what MKTRF, SMB and HML represent. Interpret the signs of the fund's SMB and HML loadings.
(b) In one month, MKTRF = 2.0%, SMB = 1.0% and HML = −0.5%. Assuming alpha and residual are zero, find the model-implied excess fund return.
(c) If that month's risk-free return is 0.2%, calculate the corresponding total fund return.
(d) A CAPM regression reports positive alpha, but a three-factor regression reports alpha close to zero. Explain how the additional factor exposures could account for this result.
(e) Distinguish a common factor return series from a firm characteristic such as book-to-market. What broad past-return pattern does the momentum factor seek to capture?

中文分析

(b) 三个乘积相加：注意 HML 这一项——因子收益 −0.5% 与载荷 −0.2 相乘得 +0.10%（负负得正），这是最容易算错的一处。
(c) 总收益 = 超额收益 + 无风险收益。

公式 \[ r-r_f = 1.1(2.0\%)+0.5(1.0\%)+(-0.2)(-0.5\%) \]
解题步骤
  1. (a) MKTRF = 市场超额收益；SMB = 小减大（规模）；HML = 高减低账面市值比（价值）。
  2. (a) 载荷解读：\(b_S=0.5>0\) → 偏小盘；\(b_H=-0.2<0\) → 偏成长（低账面市值比）。
  3. (b) 市场项：\(1.1\times2.0\%=2.20\%\)。
  4. (b) 规模项：\(0.5\times1.0\%=0.50\%\)。
  5. (b) 价值项：\((-0.2)\times(-0.5\%)=+0.10\%\)。
  6. (b) 超额收益合计 \(=2.20+0.50+0.10=\mathbf{2.80\%}\)。
  7. (c) 总收益 \(=2.80\%+0.20\%=\mathbf{3.00\%}\)。
  8. (d) CAPM 把规模与价值暴露带来的收益记入了截距 → 出现正 alpha；控制这些暴露后，剩余不可解释部分变小 → alpha 趋近 0。
  9. (e) 共同因子 = 跨资产共享的收益时间序列；账面市值比 = 随公司与时间变化的公司特征，可用来构造因子组合。动量捕捉「近期相对赢家在随后继续跑赢近期相对输家」的模式。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) MKTRF: market return less the risk-free rate. SMB: small-minus-big (size). HML: high-minus-low book-to-market (value). Positive SMB loading = small-cap tilt; negative HML loading = growth tilt.
(b) \(1.1(2.0\%)+0.5(1.0\%)+(-0.2)(-0.5\%)=\mathbf{2.80\%}\); the negative HML return contributes positively because its loading is also negative.
(c) Total return \(=2.80\%+0.20\%=\mathbf{3.00\%}\).
(d) CAPM attributes returns associated with size and value exposures to its intercept; controlling for them leaves less unexplained, so alpha falls toward zero.
(e) A common factor is a shared time series of portfolio returns; a characteristic such as book-to-market varies across firms and is used to form those portfolios. Momentum captures the tendency of recent relative winners to outperform recent relative losers.

中文答案

(a) 三个因子含义 + 载荷解读（偏小盘、偏成长）；(b) 超额收益 2.80%；(c) 总收益 3.00%；
(d) CAPM 遗漏因子 → 因子收益被记入 alpha；加入因子后 alpha 趋近 0；
(e) 因子是收益序列，特征是公司属性；动量捕捉近期赢家继续跑赢的规律。
关键陷阱：HML 项 (−0.2)×(−0.5%) = +0.10%，负负得正。

  5. (e) 因子 = 收益序列；特征 = 公司属性；动量 = 赢家继续跑赢。
EN Answer

(a) Market excess return; small-minus-big; high-minus-low B/M; small-cap and growth tilts. (b) 2.80%. (c) 3.00%. (d) CAPM attributes size/value returns to its intercept. (e) A factor is a shared return series; B/M is a firm characteristic; momentum captures recent winners outperforming recent losers.

中文答案

(a) 见分析；(b) 2.80%；(c) 3.00%；(d) 遗漏因子导致 alpha 虚高；(e) 因子 vs 特征；动量 = 赢家继续跑赢。

Q Two-stage growth with a decline 拓展
EN

A firm just paid a dividend of $1.50. Dividends are expected to grow at 12% for the next three years and then at 3% forever. The required return is 10%.
(a) Compute \(D_1\), \(D_2\), \(D_3\) and \(D_4\). (b) Terminal value at the end of Year 3. (c) Current share value.

查看解答 / Answer
中文分析

两阶段增长：先逐年算出高速期的股利，再用固定增长公式在第 3 期末求终值（分子是 \(D_4\)），最后把 \(D_1\)–\(D_3\) 与终值一起折现。

  1. \(D_1=1.50\times1.12=\$1.68\)。
  2. \(D_2=1.68\times1.12=\$1.8816\approx\$1.88\)。
  3. \(D_3=1.8816\times1.12=\$2.1074\approx\$2.11\)。
  4. \(D_4=2.1074\times1.03=\$2.1706\approx\$2.17\)。
  5. \(TV_3=D_4/(r-g)=2.1706/(0.10-0.03)=2.1706/0.07=\mathbf{\$31.01}\)。
  6. 折现：\(\dfrac{1.68}{1.10}=1.5273\)；\(\dfrac{1.8816}{1.10^2}=1.5542\)；\(\dfrac{2.1074}{1.10^3}=1.5831\)；\(\dfrac{31.01}{1.10^3}=23.2912\)。
  7. 合计：\(P_0=1.5273+1.5542+1.5831+23.2912=\mathbf{\$27.96}\)。
EN Answer

(a) \(D_1=\)\$1.68, \(D_2=\)\$1.88, \(D_3=\)\$2.11, \(D_4=\)\$2.17. (b) \(TV_3=2.1706/(0.10-0.03)=\)\$31.01. (c) \(P_0=1.68/1.10+1.8816/1.10^2+(2.1074+31.01)/1.10^3=\)\$27.96.

中文答案

(a) \(D_1\)–\(D_4\) = \$1.68 / \$1.88 / \$2.11 / \$2.17；(b) 第 3 期末终值 \$31.01；(c) 当前价值 \$27.96。
注意：终值的分子必须是 \(D_4\)（第 4 年股利），且折现率仍用 10%（\(r>g=3\%\) 满足收敛条件）。

Q CAPM required return versus the market price 拓展
EN

Stock XYZ has β = 1.30. The risk-free rate is 3.00% and the expected market return is 9.00%. Next year's dividend is expected to be ¥2.40 and dividends are expected to grow at 5.00% forever.
(a) Compute the required return on equity using the CAPM.
(b) Compute the intrinsic value using the Gordon growth model.
(c) The market price is ¥45.00. Is the stock over- or undervalued? What growth rate is the market implicitly assuming?

查看解答 / Answer
中文分析

把两条线接起来：CAPM 给出折现率 r，戈登模型把 r 变成价格。\(r=r_f+\beta\,(r_m-r_f)\)，\(V_0=\dfrac{D_1}{r-g}\)。注意使用 D1（下一期股利）而不是 D0。反解市场隐含 g 是检验估值分歧的常用手法。

  1. (a) \(r=3.00\%+1.30\times(9.00\%-3.00\%)=3.00\%+7.80\%=\) 10.80%。
  2. (b) \(V_0=\dfrac{2.40}{0.1080-0.0500}=\dfrac{2.40}{0.0580}=\) ¥41.38。
  3. (c) 市价 ¥45.00 > 内在价值 ¥41.38 → 被高估，应卖出/回避。
  4. (c) 反解：\(45.00=\dfrac{2.40}{0.1080-g}\) → \(0.1080-g=\dfrac{2.40}{45.00}=0.053333\)。
  5. (c) \(g=0.1080-0.053333=\) 5.47%。
  6. (c) 含义：市场隐含的永续增长率比你的估计高 47 bp——估值分歧最终归结为对 g 的分歧。
EN Answer

(a) r = 3.00% + 1.30 × 6.00% = 10.80%. (b) V0 = 2.40/(0.1080 − 0.0500) = ¥41.38. (c) The market price of ¥45.00 exceeds intrinsic value, so the stock is overvalued. Solving 45.00 = 2.40/(0.1080 − g) gives g = 5.47%, i.e. the market is pricing in 47 bp more growth than your estimate.

中文答案

(a) 要求回报率 10.80%。(b) 内在价值 ¥41.38。(c) 市价 ¥45 > ¥41.38，被高估；市场隐含增长率 5.47%，比假设的 5.00% 高 47 bp。

Q Payout, growth and PVGO: why high P/E can be justified 拓展
EN

Two firms both have expected earnings of ¥5.00 per share next year and a required return of 10%. Firm A pays out 80% of earnings and grows at 4%; Firm B pays out 40% and grows at 8%.
(a) Compute the price and the P/E ratio of each firm.
(b) Compute the no-growth value E1/r and the PVGO of each firm.
(c) Is Firm B's higher P/E evidence that it is overvalued? Explain.

查看解答 / Answer
中文分析

把价格拆成两块：零增长价值（现有盈利的永续年金） 与 PVGO（增长机会的现值）。高市盈率只说明市场为增长机会付了更多钱，本身不是「贵」的证据——关键看增长是否真的能实现且 ROE > r。

  1. (a) Firm A：D1 = 5.00 × 80% = ¥4.00；\(P_A=\dfrac{4.00}{0.10-0.04}=\) ¥66.67；P/E = 66.67 ÷ 5.00 = 13.33×。
  2. (a) Firm B：D1 = 5.00 × 40% = ¥2.00；\(P_B=\dfrac{2.00}{0.10-0.08}=\) ¥100.00；P/E = 100.00 ÷ 5.00 = 20.00×。
  3. (b) 零增长价值 = E1/r = 5.00 ÷ 0.10 = ¥50.00（两家相同）。
  4. (b) PVGOA = 66.67 − 50.00 = ¥16.67；PVGOB = 100.00 − 50.00 = ¥50.00。
  5. (c) 不能据此判断 B 被高估：B 的 PVGO 更高，源于它留存更多利润用于再投资并获得更高增长。
  6. (c) 判断标准应是再投资的回报率是否高于要求回报率 r：若 ROE > r，高留存创造正 PVGO；若 ROE < r，高留存反而毁灭价值。
EN Answer

(a) A: D1 = ¥4.00, P = ¥66.67, P/E = 13.33×. B: D1 = ¥2.00, P = ¥100.00, P/E = 20.00×. (b) No-growth value = 5.00/0.10 = ¥50.00 for both; PVGOA = ¥16.67, PVGOB = ¥50.00. (c) A higher P/E is not by itself evidence of overvaluation — B retains more earnings and grows faster. The test is whether reinvestment earns more than the required return: positive PVGO requires ROE > r.

中文答案

(a) A：价格 ¥66.67、P/E 13.33×；B：价格 ¥100.00、P/E 20.00×。(b) 零增长价值均为 ¥50.00；PVGOA = ¥16.67，PVGOB = ¥50.00。(c) 高 P/E 不等于高估，关键看再投资回报率是否高于 r。

Q Fama-French three-factor expected return 拓展
EN

The risk-free rate is 2.50%. Factor premiums: market 6.00%, SMB (size) 2.00%, HML (value) 3.50%. A stock has loadings βMKT = 0.90, βSMB = 0.60, βHML = −0.40.
(a) Compute the expected return using the Fama-French three-factor model.
(b) Compute the CAPM-only estimate and explain the sign and size of the difference.
(c) Interpret the stock's style: is it a small-cap growth, small-cap value, large-cap growth or large-cap value stock?

查看解答 / Answer
中文分析

三因子模型在 CAPM 之上再加两个风格因子：\(E(R)=r_f+\beta_{MKT}(R_m-r_f)+\beta_{SMB}\cdot SMB+\beta_{HML}\cdot HML\)。SMB 载荷为正 = 小盘倾向；HML 载荷为负 = 成长倾向。

  1. (a) 市场项 = 0.90 × 6.00% = 5.40%；规模项 = 0.60 × 2.00% = 1.20%；价值项 = (−0.40) × 3.50% = −1.40%。
  2. (a) \(E(R)=2.50\%+5.40\%+1.20\%-1.40\%=\) 7.70%。
  3. (b) CAPM：\(2.50\%+0.90\times6.00\%=\) 7.90%。
  4. (b) 差 7.70% − 7.90% = −0.20% (−20 bp)：小盘敞口贡献 +120 bp，成长敞口贡献 −140 bp，净 −20 bp。
  5. (c) βSMB > 0 → 小盘；βHML < 0 → 成长型（账面市值比低）。
  6. (c) 结论：这是一只小盘成长股（small-cap growth）。
EN Answer

(a) E(R) = 2.50% + 0.90 × 6.00% + 0.60 × 2.00% + (−0.40) × 3.50% = 7.70%. (b) CAPM gives 2.50% + 5.40% = 7.90%, i.e. 20 bp higher: the small-cap tilt adds 120 bp while the growth tilt subtracts 140 bp. (c) Positive SMB loading plus negative HML loading makes it a small-cap growth stock.

中文答案

(a) 预期回报 7.70%。(b) CAPM 估计 7.90%，高出 20 bp：小盘敞口 +120 bp、成长敞口 −140 bp。(c) 属于小盘成长股。
