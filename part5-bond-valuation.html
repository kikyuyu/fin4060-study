Chapter 3.1 Interest Rates and Security Valuation: Bond Pricing

Part 5 债券估值

本部分把 Part 3–4 得到的折现率代入债券：价格就是未来现金流按到期收益率折现的和。核心考点有三个——三种收益率（票息率 / 要求回报率 / 实现回报率）的区分、溢价折价平价的判断、以及付息频率改变时如何调整周期利率与期数。这些是考试计算题的最密集区域。

债券定价到期收益率 YTM溢价 / 折价 / 平价半年付息零息债浮息债 FRN拉动至面值持有期回报

1. 三种收益率必须分清 Three Rates

Chapter 3.1 · slides 3–20Practice Q10(e)
名称英文由什么决定作用
票息率Coupon rate合同约定：票息 ÷ 面值决定每期付多少钱
要求回报率Required return与该证券风险匹配的折现率用于计算公允价值
到期收益率Yield to maturity (YTM)由市场价格反解出的内部收益率衡量按市价买入能拿到多少
实现持有期回报Realized HPR实际买入价、收到的票息、实际卖出价衡量真正赚了多少
▸ 核心结论：这三者不需要相等

票息率是合同参数，发行时就固定；要求回报率随市场与风险变化；实现回报率取决于你实际的买卖时点与价格。只有在极特殊的情形下（按公允价值买入并持有到期、无违约、票息按 YTM 再投资），三者才会一致。
答题句式：The coupon rate determines the contractual payment; the required return is the discount rate appropriate for risk; the realized return uses the actual purchase price, coupons and sale proceeds, so it can differ from both.

▸ Definition：到期收益率

Yield to maturity (YTM): the single discount rate that equates the bond's current market price to the present value of all its remaining promised cash flows (coupons plus face value at redemption).

债券定价通式 \[ P=\sum_{t=1}^{T}\frac{CF_t}{(1+y)^t}=\sum_{t=1}^{T}\frac{C}{(1+y)^t}+\frac{F}{(1+y)^T} \]
\(P\) = 价格；\(C\) = 每期票息（\(C=c\times F\)，\(c\) 为票息率）；\(F\) = 面值 face value；\(y\) = 到期收益率 / 要求回报率；\(T\) = 剩余期数。
▸ 三个最常见的计算错误
  1. 折现用错利率：用票息率折现是错的，必须用要求回报率 / YTM。
  2. 末期现金流漏掉本金：最后一期是 \(C+F\)（如 \$1,060），不是只有票息。
  3. 判断低估/高估时拿面值当基准：比较对象是你算出的公允价值，不是面值。

2. 溢价、折价与平价 Premium / Discount / Par

Chapter 3.1 · slides 21–25, 29–46Practice Q10, Q11(c)
▸ 判定规则（一条就够）
  • \(y < c\)（收益率低于票息率）→ 价格 > 面值 → 溢价 premium；
  • \(y = c\) → 价格 = 面值 → 平价 par；
  • \(y > c\) → 价格 < 面值 → 折价 discount。

直觉：票息率高于市场要求时，投资者愿意多付一点钱来拿到这份「高于市场」的票息，于是价格被推到面值之上。

▸ 拉动至面值 Pull to par

收益率不变且承诺现金流不变时，随着到期日临近，溢价幅度会逐渐收敛到 0（折价同理向上收敛）。比较「每次付息后的价格」可以剔除票息累积的干扰，看到纯粹的收敛趋势。注意：票息本身仍然是投资者总回报的一部分。

EXAMPLE 1 Bond value, under/overpricing, and realized HPR
EN

A default-free bond has a face value of $1,000, pays a 6% annual coupon and matures in three years. The next coupon is exactly one year away. The required annual return is 5%.
(a) List the bond's cash flows and calculate its fair value.
(b) The market price is $1,015. Compare it with your valuation and explain whether the bond is underpriced or overpriced.
(c) Without solving for the yield numerically, explain whether the yield implied by $1,015 is above or below 5%.
(d) An investor pays $1,015 and sells the bond for $990 immediately after receiving the first $60 coupon. Calculate the one-year realized holding-period return.
(e) Explain why the coupon rate, required return and realized holding-period return need not be equal.

中文分析

(a) 现金流：第 1、2 年 \$60，第 3 年 \$1,060。按 5% 折现求和。
(b) 市场价 \$1,015 低于公允价值 → 被低估（按此价买入能获得高于 5% 的回报）。
(c) 用「价格与收益率反向」定性判断，不必解方程。
(d) 实现回报 =（收到的票息 + 卖出价 − 买入价）÷ 买入价。注意分子要包含票息，且分母是买入价。

公式 \[ P=\frac{60}{1.05}+\frac{60}{1.05^2}+\frac{1{,}060}{1.05^3} \qquad HPR=\frac{\text{Coupon}+P_{sale}-P_{buy}}{P_{buy}} \]
解题步骤
  1. (a) 现金流：\(CF_1=60\)，\(CF_2=60\)，\(CF_3=60+1{,}000=1{,}060\)。
  2. (a) 折现：\(60/1.05=57.14\)；\(60/1.05^2=54.42\)；\(1{,}060/1.05^3=915.67\)。
  3. (a) 求和：\(P=\mathbf{\$1{,}027.23}\)。
  4. (b) \(1{,}027.23-1{,}015.00=12.23\) → 市场价更低 → 被低估 \$12.23。
  5. (c) 现金流为正时价格与收益率反向；\(\$1{,}015<\$1{,}027.23\) → 隐含收益率 高于 5%。
  6. (d) \(HPR=(60+990-1{,}015)/1{,}015=35/1{,}015=3.4483\%\approx\mathbf{3.45\%}\)。
  7. (d) 拆解：票息贡献 \(60/1{,}015=5.91\%\)，资本损失 \((990-1{,}015)/1{,}015=-2.46\%\)，合计 3.45%。
  8. (e) 票息率由合同定；要求回报率由风险定；实现回报率由实际买卖价与票息定 → 三者不必相等。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) \(CF_1=CF_2=\$60\), \(CF_3=\$1{,}060\); \(P=60/1.05+60/1.05^2+1{,}060/1.05^3=\mathbf{\$1{,}027.23}\).
(b) Underpriced by \$12.23 relative to the stated valuation.
(c) The price is below the value at 5%, and price falls as yield rises, so the implied yield is above 5%.
(d) \(HPR=(60+990-1{,}015)/1{,}015=\mathbf{3.45\%}\), including coupon income and the capital loss.
(e) The coupon rate sets the contractual payment; the required return is the risk-appropriate discount rate; the realized return uses actual prices, coupons and proceeds, so it can differ from both.

中文答案

(a) 公允价值 \$1,027.23；(b) 市场价 \$1,015 使其被低估 \$12.23；(c) 隐含收益率高于 5%；(d) 一年实现回报 3.45%。
(e) 三者由不同因素决定，不必相等。
本题的陷阱在 (d)：实现回报低于票息率 6%，因为卖出价低于买入价产生了资本损失。

3. 付息频率、零息债与浮息债 Frequency, Zeros & Floaters

Chapter 3.1 · slides 21–25, 29–46Practice Q11
▸ 付息频率调整的三步
  1. 每期票息 = 年票息 ÷ \(m\)（\(m\) = 每年付息次数）；
  2. 每期折现率 = 报价年收益率 ÷ \(m\)；
  3. 总期数 = 剩余年数 × \(m\)。

半年度付息（\(m=2\)）是最常考的情形。报价的 YTM 是名义年利率，不是有效年利率；若要比较，需换算为 \((1+y/2)^2-1\)。

半年付息定价 \[ P=\sum_{t=1}^{2T}\frac{C/2}{(1+y/2)^t}+\frac{F}{(1+y/2)^{2T}} \qquad \text{EAY}=\left(1+\frac{y}{2}\right)^2-1 \]
▸ 零息债 Zero-coupon bond

不付票息，只到期兑付面值：\(P=F/(1+y)^T\)。投资者的回报全部来自购买时的折价——低价买入、到期收回面值。

▸ 浮息债 Floating-rate note (FRN)

票息 = 参考利率 + 利差（spread），在每个重置日（reset date）重新确定。因为票息随市场利率走，其利率风险显著低于同期限固息债。

EXAMPLE 2 Semiannual coupon, zero-coupon and floaters
EN

Bond A has a face value of $1,000, a 6% annual coupon paid semiannually, and two years to maturity. Its quoted annual yield is 4%, compounded semiannually. Valuation occurs on a coupon date just after payment.
(a) Calculate the coupon per half-year, periodic discount rate, number of remaining payments and price of Bond A.
(b) Calculate the price of a two-year zero-coupon bond with the same face value and quoted yield. Explain how a zero-coupon investor earns a return without receiving coupons.
(c) For Bond A, explain whether its price would be above, equal to, or below par if its quoted yield were 6% or 8%, with the same compounding convention.
(d) A floating-rate note resets its annual coupon to the reference rate plus 1 percentage point. Calculate the coupon rate when the reference rate is 3% and when it is 4%. Contrast this with Bond A's fixed coupon.
(e) Explain the assumptions needed for a coupon bond's YTM to equal the investor's compound annual return over the full holding period to maturity.

中文分析

(a) 半年付息：每期票息 \$30、每期折现率 2%、共 4 期。四项现金流折现求和。
(c) 用「\(y\) 与 \(c\) 比较」的规则：报价收益率 6% 时，周期利率 3% = 半年票息率 3% → 平价；8% 时高于票息率 → 折价。

公式 \[ P_A=\frac{30}{1.02}+\frac{30}{1.02^2}+\frac{30}{1.02^3}+\frac{1{,}030}{1.02^4} \qquad P_{zero}=\frac{1{,}000}{1.02^4} \]
解题步骤
  1. (a) 每期票息：\(1{,}000\times6\%/2=\$30\)。
  2. (a) 周期折现率：\(4\%/2=2\%\)；剩余期数：\(2\times2=4\)。
  3. (a) 折现：\(30/1.02=29.41\)；\(30/1.02^2=28.84\)；\(30/1.02^3=28.27\)；\(1{,}030/1.02^4=951.56\)。
  4. (a) 求和：\(P_A=\mathbf{\$1{,}038.08}\)（溢价，因为 \(y=4\%<c=6\%\)）。
  5. (b) 零息债：\(1{,}000/1.02^4=\mathbf{\$923.85}\)。
  6. (b) 回报来源：以低于面值的价格买入，到期收回 \$1,000，差额 \$76.15 即为回报。
  7. (c) 报价 6%：周期 3% = 半年票息率 3% → 平价 \$1,000；报价 8%：周期 4% > 3% → 折价（低于 \$1,000）。
  8. (d) FRN：参考 3% → 票息 4%；参考 4% → 票息 5%；而 Bond A 的票息恒为 6%。
  9. (e) 假设：持有至到期、所有承诺现金流按约支付（无违约、无提前赎回）、且期间票息能以 YTM 再投资；半年报价时还要区分名义 YTM 与有效年收益。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) Semiannual coupon = \$30; periodic yield = 2%; remaining payments = 4; \(P_A=30/1.02+30/1.02^2+30/1.02^3+1{,}030/1.02^4=\mathbf{\$1{,}038.08}\).
(b) \(P_{zero}=1{,}000/1.02^4=\mathbf{\$923.85}\). The return comes from buying below face value and receiving \$1,000 at maturity.
(c) At a quoted 6% the bond is at par; at 8% it is below par, because the fixed 6% coupon cannot deliver the market yield without a purchase discount.
(d) The floating coupon is 4% at a 3% reference rate and 5% at 4%, once reset; Bond A's contractual coupon remains 6%.
(e) The investor must hold to maturity, receive all promised payments without default or early call, and reinvest interim coupons at the YTM. For semiannual quotations, compare annual compound returns with \((1+y/2)^2-1\).

中文答案

(a) 每期 \$30、周期 2%、4 期，价格 \$1,038.08；(b) 零息债 \$923.85，收益来自折价；
(c) 报价 6% → 平价，报价 8% → 折价；(d) FRN 票息 4% / 5%，Bond A 恒为 6%；
(e) 需持有到期、无违约/提前赎回、票息按 YTM 再投资。
易错点：报价 8% 时容易误以为周期利率是 8%，正确是 4%/期。

Q Solving for YTM by trial and interpolation 拓展
EN

A two-year bond with F = $1,000 and a 5% annual coupon trades at $977.50. The next coupon is one year away.
(a) Is the YTM above or below 5%, and why?
(b) Compute the price at 6% and at 7%, then estimate the YTM by linear interpolation.

查看解答 / Answer
中文分析

价格低于面值 → 折价 → \(y>c=5\%\)。插值法：在两个试算收益率之间，价格随收益率近似线性变化，用价格差的比例推算收益率。

  1. (a) 价格 \$977.50 < 面值 → 折价 → YTM 高于 5%。
  2. (b) 6% 时：\(P=50/1.06+1{,}050/1.06^2=47.17+934.50=\$981.67\)。
  3. (b) 7% 时：\(P=50/1.07+1{,}050/1.07^2=46.73+917.29=\$964.02\)。
  4. 目标价 \$977.50 位于 981.67 与 964.02 之间。
  5. 比例：\(\dfrac{981.67-977.50}{981.67-964.02}=\dfrac{4.17}{17.65}=0.2363\)。
  6. 估计 \(YTM\approx6\%+0.2363\times1\%=\mathbf{6.24\%}\)（精确解约 6.23%）。
EN Answer

(a) Above 5%: the bond trades below par. (b) \(P(6\%)=\$981.67\), \(P(7\%)=\$964.02\). Interpolating: \(6\%+\frac{981.67-977.50}{981.67-964.02}\times1\%\approx\mathbf{6.24\%}\).

中文答案

(a) 高于 5%（折价交易）。(b) 6% → \$981.67，7% → \$964.02，插值估计 YTM ≈ 6.24%。插值只是近似，因为价格—收益率关系是弯的（凸性）。

溢价债券（票息率 > YTM）的价格会沿着到期日「被拉回面值」（pull to par），所以资本利得为负。总回报 = 当期收益率 + 资本利得率；当收益率不变时，这个总回报恰好等于 YTM。

  2. (a) \(P_0=8\times4.212364+100\times0.747258=33.6989+74.7258=\) ¥108.42。
  3. (a) 4 年期：\((1-1.06^{-4})/0.06=(1-0.792094)/0.06=3.465106\)。
  4. (a) \(P_1=8\times3.465106+100\times0.792094=27.7208+79.2094=\) ¥106.93。
  5. (b) 当期收益率 = 8 ÷ 108.4247 = 7.378%。
  6. (b) 资本利得率 = (106.9302 − 108.4247) ÷ 108.4247 = −1.4945 ÷ 108.4247 = −1.378%。
  7. (c) 7.378% + (−1.378%) = 6.000% = YTM ✓。资本利得为负是因为它是溢价债券，价格必然向 ¥100 回落。
EN Answer

(a) P0 = 8 × 4.212364 + 100 × 0.747258 = ¥108.42; P1 = 8 × 3.465106 + 100 × 0.792094 = ¥106.93. (b) Current yield = 8/108.4247 = 7.378%; capital gain yield = (106.9302 − 108.4247)/108.4247 = −1.378%. (c) 7.378% − 1.378% = 6.000% = the YTM. The capital gain is negative because a premium bond's price is pulled toward par as maturity approaches.

(a) P0 = ¥108.42，P1 = ¥106.93。(b) 当期收益率 7.378%，资本利得率 −1.378%。(c) 两者相加 = 6.000% = YTM；资本利得为负源于溢价债券价格被拉回面值。

Q Semiannual coupons and compounding frequency 拓展
EN

A 3-year bond with a 6% coupon rate and ¥100 face, YTM quoted at 5%.
(a) If coupons are paid semiannually, compute the price.
(b) Compute the effective annual yield in that case.
(c) If instead the coupon were paid annually with the same 5% YTM, compute the price. Which price is higher, and why?

查看解答 / Answer
中文分析

处理半年付息债券的三步：票息减半、期数加倍、收益率减半。报价的 YTM 是 APR（名义年利率），必须除以 2 才能作为每期折现率。

  1. (a) 每期票息 = 6% × 100 ÷ 2 = ¥3；期数 = 3 × 2 = 6；每期折现率 = 5% ÷ 2 = 2.5%。
  2. (a) 年金因子 \((1-1.025^{-6})/0.025=(1-0.862297)/0.025=5.508125\)。
  3. (a) \(P=3\times5.508125+100\times0.862297=16.5244+86.2297=\) ¥102.75。
  4. (b) \(\text{EAR}=(1.025)^{2}-1=\) 5.0625%。
  5. (c) 年付息：年金因子 \((1-1.05^{-3})/0.05=(1-0.863838)/0.05=2.723248\)。
  6. (c) \(P=6\times2.723248+100\times0.863838=16.3395+86.3838=\) ¥102.72。
  7. (c) 半年付息价格更高（102.75 > 102.72），因为半年的票息可以更早收到并再投资，即复利频率更高。
EN Answer

(a) Half-year coupon ¥3, 6 periods, 2.5% per period: P = 3 × 5.508125 + 100 × 0.862297 = ¥102.75. (b) EAR = (1.025)2 − 1 = 5.0625%. (c) Annual coupon: P = 6 × 2.723248 + 100 × 0.863838 = ¥102.72. The semiannual price is higher because coupons arrive earlier and can be reinvested sooner — more frequent compounding.

中文答案

(a) ¥102.75。(b) EAR = 5.0625%。(c) 年付息 ¥102.72；半年付息更高，因为票息更早到手、复利频率更高。
