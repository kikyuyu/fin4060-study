1. 四种收益率报价 Yield Conventions

Chapter 5a · slides 2–24Practice Q22
▸ 天数约定（全章第一纪律）
  • 折现收益率与单利收益率：使用 360 天年；
  • BEY 与 EAR：使用 365 天年。

练习集的统一约定：Money-market discount and single-payment quotations use a 360-day year. Bond equivalent yields (BEY) and effective annual returns (EAR) use 365 days. 答题时先写下天数假设，能避免绝大多数失分。

持有期回报 \[ HPR=\frac{F-P}{P}=\frac{D}{P} \]
\(F\) = 面值 / 到期收到的金额；\(P\) = 购买价格；\(D=F-P\) = 折价（dollar discount）。这是实际赚到的回报率，其余四种只是对它的不同年化方式。
折现收益率（分母用面值，360 天） \[ r_D=\frac{F-P}{F}\times\frac{360}{n} \]
单利收益率（分母用投资额，360 天） \[ r_{SPY}=\frac{F-P}{P}\times\frac{360}{n} \]
债券等价收益率 BEY（线性年化，365 天） \[ BEY=\frac{F-P}{P}\times\frac{365}{n} \]
实际年化收益率 EAR（复利年化，365 天） \[ EAR=(1+HPR)^{365/n}-1=\left(\frac{F}{P}\right)^{365/n}-1 \]
▸ 两条永远成立的排序
  1. \(r_D < r_{SPY}\)：折现收益率的分母是面值（更大），所以数值更小。
  2. \(r_{SPY} < BEY < EAR\)：365 > 360（线性年化更大），且复利年化 > 线性年化。

合起来：\(r_D < r_{SPY} < BEY < EAR\)。算完可以用这条排序自检。

▸ 报价年化率 ≠ 实际赚到的钱

年化只是把这段持有期的回报折算成年度口径以便比较；90 天真正到手的是 \(F-P\) 这笔美元金额，不是把年化率乘一年得到的数。要真正实现 EAR，还需要有合适的再投资机会。

EXAMPLE 1 Converting among the four quotations
EN

A 90-day discount security has a face value of $100,000 and a purchase price of $98,800. Discount and single-payment yields use a 360-day year; BEY uses a 365-day year and the purchase price as its base.
(a) Calculate the dollar gain and the 90-day holding-period return if the security pays full face value at maturity.
(b) Calculate its discount yield and its single-payment yield. Explain why the two quoted rates differ.
(c) Calculate its BEY and EAR, using \((1+HPR)^{365/90}\) for EAR.
(d) Explain why a quoted annualized rate is not the dollar return actually earned over 90 days.
(e) Explain why maturity, liquidity and credit quality matter when a firm selects an instrument for temporarily idle cash, even if one instrument offers a higher quoted yield.

中文分析

先算 \(D=F-P=1{,}200\)，然后四个公式逐一代入。关键区别只有两点：分母是 F 还是 P，以及年用 360 还是 365 天。

公式 \[ r_D=\frac{1{,}200}{100{,}000}\cdot\frac{360}{90} \qquad r_{SPY}=\frac{1{,}200}{98{,}800}\cdot\frac{360}{90} \qquad BEY=\frac{1{,}200}{98{,}800}\cdot\frac{365}{90} \]
解题步骤
  1. (a) 美元收益 \(D=100{,}000-98{,}800=\mathbf{\$1{,}200}\)。
  2. (a) \(HPR=1{,}200/98{,}800=1.2146\%\approx\mathbf{1.21\%}\)。
  3. (b) \(r_D=(1{,}200/100{,}000)\times(360/90)=0.012\times4=\mathbf{4.80\%}\)。
  4. (b) \(r_{SPY}=(1{,}200/98{,}800)\times4=0.0121457\times4=\mathbf{4.86\%}\)。
  5. (b) 差异原因：折现收益率用面值作分母（分母更大 → 比率更小），单利用实际投资额作分母。
  6. (c) \(BEY=0.0121457\times(365/90)=0.0121457\times4.05556=\mathbf{4.93\%}\)。
  7. (c) \(EAR=(100{,}000/98{,}800)^{365/90}-1=(1.0121457)^{4.05556}-1=\mathbf{5.02\%}\)。
  8. 自检：\(4.80<4.86<4.93<5.02\) ✓ 符合排序规律。
  9. (d) 年化只是换算口径，90 天实际到手的是 \$1,200。
  10. (e) 选择闲置资金工具的顺序：先匹配期限（现金何时要用），再看流动性（是否需要提前变现），最后才比报价收益率；信用质量影响能否收回本金。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) Dollar gain = \$1,200; \(HPR=1{,}200/98{,}800=\mathbf{1.21\%}\).
(b) \(r_D=(1{,}200/100{,}000)(360/90)=\mathbf{4.80\%}\); \(r_{SPY}=(1{,}200/98{,}800)(360/90)=\mathbf{4.86\%}\). The discount yield uses face value rather than invested funds as its denominator.
(c) \(BEY=(1{,}200/98{,}800)(365/90)=\mathbf{4.93\%}\); \(EAR=(100{,}000/98{,}800)^{365/90}-1=\mathbf{5.02\%}\). EAR compounds the holding-period return; BEY annualises it linearly.
(d) Annualisation rescales the 90-day return; the holding period actually produces \$1,200. Realising the EAR requires suitable reinvestment opportunities.
(e) Maturity should match the cash-use horizon; liquidity matters if early sale is needed; credit quality affects repayment. A higher quoted rate may compensate for risk or use a different convention, so rates must be made comparable.

中文答案

(a) \$1,200 / 1.21%；(b) 4.80% / 4.86%（分母不同）；(c) 4.93% / 5.02%；
(d) 年化率 ≠ 90 天实际赚到的钱；(e) 期限 → 流动性 → 收益率，顺序不能颠倒。
自检排序：4.80 < 4.86 < 4.93 < 5.02 ✓

2. 国库券：报价与拍卖 Treasury Bills

Chapter 5b · slides 3–24Practice Q23, Q24
▸ Definition

Treasury bill (T-bill): a short-term government security sold at a discount to face value and redeemed at full face value at maturity. It pays no coupon; the return is the purchase discount.
Bid discount yield / ask discount yield: the dealer's discount-yield quotation on the side it buys (bid) and the side it sells (ask).

▸ 反向关系：折现收益率越高 → 价格越低

价格公式是 \(P=F\big[1-r_D\,(n/360)\big]\)。折现收益率 \(r_D\) 越大，折扣越大，价格越低。因此交易商的买入价（bid）低于卖出价（ask），而对应的买入折现收益率高于卖出折现收益率。
客户买入国库券时按交易商的 ask（卖出价）成交。

EXAMPLE 2 Dealer quotes and the bid-ask spread
EN

A dealer quotes a 90-day Treasury bill with face value $100,000 at a bid discount yield of 4.20% and an ask discount yield of 4.10%. Both use a 360-day year.
(a) Calculate the dealer's bid price and ask price.
(b) Which price does a customer pay to buy the bill? Calculate that customer's BEY if the bill is held to maturity, using a 365-day year.
(c) Calculate the dollar bid-ask spread. Explain why the bid discount yield is higher than the ask discount yield even though the bid price is lower.
(d) Explain why a Treasury bill is usually highly liquid and has low credit risk. Why does this not imply that its price can never change before maturity?

中文分析

(a) 用 \(P=F[1-r_D(n/360)]\)，其中 \(n/360=90/360=0.25\)。(b) 客户买入按 ask；(c) 价差 = ask − bid。
关键解释：折现收益率与价格反向，所以「bid 价更低」与「bid 收益率更高」是同一件事的两种说法。

公式 \[ P=F\left[1-r_D\cdot\frac{n}{360}\right] \qquad BEY=\frac{F-P}{P}\cdot\frac{365}{n} \]
解题步骤
  1. (a) Bid 价 \(=100{,}000[1-0.042\times0.25]=100{,}000\times0.9895=\mathbf{\$98{,}950.00}\)。
  2. (a) Ask 价 \(=100{,}000[1-0.041\times0.25]=100{,}000\times0.98975=\mathbf{\$98{,}975.00}\)。
  3. (b) 客户买入按交易商 ask = \$98,975.00。
  4. (b) \(BEY=\dfrac{100{,}000-98{,}975}{98{,}975}\times\dfrac{365}{90}=0.0103563\times4.05556=\mathbf{4.20\%}\)。
  5. (c) 价差 \(=98{,}975.00-98{,}950.00=\mathbf{\$25.00}\)。
  6. (c) 解释：折现报价下收益率越高折扣越大、价格越低，因此「bid 收益率 4.20% > ask 收益率 4.10%」与「bid 价 98,950 < ask 价 98,975」完全一致。
  7. (d) 高流动性 + 低信用风险的原因：政府信用背书（低违约风险）、交易活跃、期限短 → 利率风险也较低。
  8. (d) 但价格仍会变动：到期前市场收益率变化会改变其转售价。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) Bid \(=100{,}000[1-0.042(90/360)]=\mathbf{\$98{,}950.00}\); ask \(=100{,}000[1-0.041(90/360)]=\mathbf{\$98{,}975.00}\).
(b) The customer buys at the dealer's ask price of \$98,975; \(BEY=[(100{,}000-98{,}975)/98{,}975](365/90)=\mathbf{4.20\%}\).
(c) Spread \(=98{,}975-98{,}950=\mathbf{\$25.00}\). Under a discount quote a larger discount yield means a lower cash price, so the higher bid yield corresponds to the lower bid price.
(d) Treasury backing supports low credit risk; extensive trading and short maturity support liquidity and relatively low interest-rate risk. Before maturity, changes in market yields can still change the sale price.

中文答案

(a) bid \$98,950.00、ask \$98,975.00；(b) 按 ask 成交，BEY 4.20%；(c) 价差 \$25.00，折现报价下收益率与价格反向；
(d) 国债背书 + 交易活跃 + 期限短 → 低风险高流动性，但到期前价格仍会随市场收益率变化。

2.1 单一价格拍卖 Single-price Auction

▸ 拍卖规则（课件口径）
  1. 发行总额中先扣掉非竞争性投标（valid noncompetitive bids），剩余部分给竞争性投标；
  2. 竞争性投标按折现收益率由低到高排序（等价于价格由高到低）；
  3. 依次分配，最后一个被接受的投标可以部分分配；
  4. 所有中标者支付同一个价格——即由最高的被接受折现收益率（stop-out yield）对应的价格；
  5. 非竞争性投标者放弃报价权，接受拍卖决定的价格，但获得全额分配。
EXAMPLE 3 A single-price Treasury bill auction
EN

A hypothetical auction offers $120 million of 90-day bills at face value. Valid noncompetitive bids total $20 million. All successful bidders pay the price associated with the highest accepted discount yield.
Bids: A — $30m at 3.80%; B — $40m at 3.90%; C — $50m at 4.00%; D — $20m at 4.10%.
(a) Amount available to competitive bidders, and the order of acceptance.
(b) Each bidder's accepted amount and the stop-out discount yield (partial allocation allowed).
(c) Common price per $100 face value (360-day year). What do noncompetitive bidders pay?
(d) What do noncompetitive bidders give up in exchange for accepting the auction-determined price?

中文分析

先减去非竞争部分 → 竞争性可用 \$100M；按收益率从低到高依次分配（A→B→C→D），C 只能拿到剩余的 \$30M（部分分配）。Stop-out 是最高的被接受收益率 = C 的 4.00%。共同价格由 4.00% 算出。

解题步骤
  1. (a) 竞争性可用 \(=120-20=\mathbf{\$100}\) 百万。
  2. (a) 接受顺序（收益率低 → 高，即价格高 → 低）：A → B → C → D。
  3. (b) A 分配 \$30M（全额）；B 分配 \$40M（全额）。
  4. (b) 剩余 \(100-30-40=\$30M\) 给 C（申报 \$50M）→ C 分配 \$30M（部分）。
  5. (b) D 未中标（\$0）。
  6. (b) Stop-out 折现收益率 = 最高的被接受收益率 = 4.00%。
  7. (c) 共同价格（每 \$100 面值）\(=100[1-0.04\times(90/360)]=100\times0.99=\mathbf{\$99.00}\)。
  8. (c) 非竞争性投标者付同样的 \$99.00（单一价格规则）。
  9. (d) 他们放弃的是指定自己愿意接受的收益率/价格的权利；其有效申请在竞争性投标决定剩余分配之前就已被满足，因此获得全额分配但接受拍卖价格，没有特殊低价。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) Competitive allocation \(=120-20=\mathbf{\$100m}\). Ranked from lowest discount yield to highest: A, B, C, D (highest price first).
(b) A gets \$30m; B gets \$40m; C gets \$30m (partial); D gets zero. After A and B only \$30m remains for C's \$50m bid. Stop-out yield = 4.00%.
(c) Common price \(=100[1-0.04(90/360)]=\mathbf{\$99.00}\) per \$100 face value; noncompetitive bidders pay this same price.
(d) They give up specifying the yield or price they will accept. Their valid requests are filled before competitive bidding determines the residual allocation, and they accept the resulting price; they do not receive a special lower price.

中文答案

(a) 竞争性可用 \$100M，顺序 A→B→C→D；(b) A 30M、B 40M、C 30M（部分）、D 0，stop-out = 4.00%；(c) 共同价格 \$99.00 / \$100，非竞争投标者付同样价格；(d) 他们放弃报价权，换取全额分配。
易错点：把 D 也算成中标，或误以为非竞争投标者拿到更低的特殊价格。

▸ 折减率 Haircut

回购中融出的现金低于抵押品市值，这个差额比例就是折减率。它为抵押品价格下跌与变现成本提供缓冲，但不消除全部风险：借款人违约、抵押品价格变动、变现困难都可能造成损失。

EXAMPLE 4 Federal funds and repo calculations
EN

Bank A lends Bank B $10 million overnight in the federal funds market at a quoted annual rate of 3.60%. Separately, a dealer receives $10 million through a seven-day repo and agrees to repay $10,007,000.
(a) Calculate the overnight interest and total repayment on the federal funds loan using a 360-day year.
(b) Identify which bank records federal funds sold and which records federal funds purchased. Explain the asset-versus-liability distinction.
(c) Calculate the repo's quoted annualized rate using a 360-day year.
(d) Identify the repo borrower and lender. Explain why the same transaction is a repo for one party and a reverse repo for the other.
(e) Compare the role of collateral in the two transactions. Explain why a haircut may be required and why collateral does not necessarily eliminate all loss risk.

中文分析

(a)(c) 都是「利息 ÷ 本金 × 360/n」的简单利率计算，注意天数：隔夜用 1/360，7 天用 360/7。
(b) 记账名称跟随方向：拆出方（贷款人）记 sold（资产），拆入方记 purchased（负债）。

公式 \[ I=P\times r\times\frac{n}{360} \qquad r_{repo}=\frac{\text{Repay}-\text{Principal}}{\text{Principal}}\times\frac{360}{n} \]
解题步骤
  1. (a) 利息 \(=10{,}000{,}000\times0.036\times\dfrac{1}{360}=\mathbf{\$1{,}000}\)。
  2. (a) 还款总额 \(=10{,}000{,}000+1{,}000=\mathbf{\$10{,}001{,}000}\)。
  3. (b) Bank A（贷款人）记录 federal funds sold（资产）；Bank B（借款人）记录 federal funds purchased（负债）。
  4. (c) 利息 \(=10{,}007{,}000-10{,}000{,}000=\$7{,}000\)。
  5. (c) \(r_{repo}=\dfrac{7{,}000}{10{,}000{,}000}\times\dfrac{360}{7}=0.0007\times51.4286=\mathbf{3.60\%}\)。
  6. (d) 收到 \$10M 的交易商是借款人（现在卖券、以后买回）→ 记为 repo；提供现金的一方是贷款人 → 记为 reverse repo。
  7. (e) 联邦基金无抵押；回购有抵押。
  8. (e) 折减率的作用：融出金额低于抵押品市值，为价格下跌与变现成本提供缓冲。
  9. (e) 为什么不消除全部风险：违约、抵押品价格变动、变现困难仍可能造成损失。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) Interest \(=10{,}000{,}000\times0.036\times(1/360)=\mathbf{\$1{,}000}\); total repayment \(\mathbf{\$10{,}001{,}000}\).
(b) Bank A, the lender, records federal funds sold (an asset); Bank B, the borrower, records federal funds purchased (a liability).
(c) \(r=(7{,}000/10{,}000{,}000)(360/7)=\mathbf{3.60\%}\).
(d) The dealer receiving \$10m is the borrower and takes the repo position (sell now, repurchase later); the cash provider is the lender and takes the reverse repo position.
(e) The federal funds loan is unsecured; the repo is collateralised. A haircut provides a buffer against price declines and liquidation costs, but default, collateral-price changes and difficulties realising collateral can still generate losses.

中文答案

(a) 利息 \$1,000，还款 \$10,001,000；(b) A 记 sold（资产）、B 记 purchased（负债）；(c) 回购利率 3.60%；
(d) 交易商 = 借款人（repo），现金提供方 = 贷款人（reverse repo）；
(e) 无抵押 vs 有抵押；折减率提供缓冲但不消除全部风险。

▸ 欧洲美元 ≠ 欧元

欧洲美元（Eurodollar）指的是存放在美国境外的美元存款（包括美国银行在境外的分行）。它既不限于欧洲，也与欧元无关——新加坡的美元存款同样是欧洲美元存款。
课件补充：LIBOR 因报价丑闻退出后，由以回购市场为基础的 SOFR 接替成为主要美元参考利率。

EXAMPLE 5 Commercial paper yields
EN

A highly rated company issues 120-day commercial paper with face value $1,000,000 at a price of $986,000 to finance seasonal inventory.
(a) Explain why commercial paper is suitable for this funding need and why strong credit quality matters.
(b) Calculate its discount yield on a 360-day basis and its BEY on a 365-day basis.
(c) Explain why a corporate issuer may obtain cheaper funding through CP than through a bank loan.
(d) An investor may need cash before the paper matures. Explain the concern and compare with T-bill liquidity.
(e) Explain why the calculated yield assumes full repayment and should not be interpreted as a guaranteed return.

中文分析

(b) 折现收益率用 360 天、分母 F；BEY 用 365 天、分母 P。注意 \(n=120\)。

解题步骤
  1. (a) 适用性：季节性库存是临时性资金需求，与 CP 的短期限匹配。
  2. (a) 信用的重要性：普通 CP 无抵押，偿付完全依赖发行人信用。
  3. (b) 折扣 \(=1{,}000{,}000-986{,}000=\$14{,}000\)。
  4. (b) \(r_D=(14{,}000/1{,}000{,}000)\times(360/120)=0.014\times3=\mathbf{4.20\%}\)。
  5. (b) \(BEY=(14{,}000/986{,}000)\times(365/120)=0.0141988\times3.041667=\mathbf{4.32\%}\)。
  6. (c) 优质发行人可直接面向投资者融资，绕过银行贷款中内含的一部分中介成本 → 可能更便宜（但不保证）。
  7. (d) 提前变现的顾虑：CP 二级市场有限，可能找不到买家或需折价出售；国库券市场活跃得多。
  8. (e) 计算假设按期全额偿付；违约、延期或不利价格提前卖出都会降低实际回报 → 不是保证收益。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) Seasonal inventory is a temporary need matching a short maturity. Ordinary CP is unsecured, so repayment relies on the issuer's creditworthiness.
(b) Discount = \$14,000. \(r_D=(14{,}000/1{,}000{,}000)(360/120)=\mathbf{4.20\%}\); \(BEY=(14{,}000/986{,}000)(365/120)=\mathbf{4.32\%}\).
(c) A strong issuer can access investors directly, potentially avoiding part of the intermediation cost embedded in a bank loan — though not guaranteed.
(d) CP has a limited secondary market; an early seller may find no ready buyer or must discount. T-bills are generally more actively traded.

  7. (b) 商业票据更便宜：5.889% − 5.342% = 约 55 bp（90 天节省约 ¥30,400）。
  8. (c) 仍选银行的理由：① 商业票据需要滚动续发，市场紧张时可能发不出去（滚续风险）；② 银行提供授信额度与备用流动性，且金额与期限更灵活；③ 票据发行有最低规模与信息披露要求，小企业难以进入。
EN Answer

(a) CP: discount = ¥260,000, usable funds = ¥19,740,000, annualised cost = (260,000/19,740,000) × 365/90 = 5.342%. Bank: borrow 20,000,000/0.90 = ¥22,222,222, interest = ¥290,411, annualised cost on usable funds = 5.889%. (b) CP is cheaper by about 55 bp. (c) A firm may still prefer the bank because CP must be rolled over (rollover risk if the market closes), the bank provides a committed credit line and backup liquidity, and CP issuance has minimum size and disclosure requirements.

中文答案

(a) 商业票据成本 5.342%，银行贷款 5.889%。(b) 票据便宜约 55 bp。(c) 仍可能选银行：票据需滚动续发存在滚续风险、银行提供授信与备用流动性、且票据发行有规模与披露门槛。
