Part 4 利率的期限结构

1. 收益率曲线与三个经验事实 The Yield Curve

Chapter 2.2 · slides 3–23HW1 Q2
▸ Definition

Term structure of interest rates / yield curve: the relationship between yield to maturity and time to maturity for a set of otherwise comparable (typically default-free government) bonds at a point in time.
Term spread（期限价差）: the difference between a long-term yield and a short-term yield, commonly \(s_t = y_{10,t} - y_{1,t}\).

期限价差与形状判定 \[ s_t \equiv y_{10,t}-y_{1,t} \]
\(s_t>0.20\%\) → 向上倾斜 upward-sloping；
\(-0.20\%\le s_t\le 0.20\%\) → 大致平坦 approximately flat；
\(s_t<-0.20\%\) → 倒挂 inverted。
（课件与 Homework 1 使用的判定规则）
▸ 三个经验事实（任何理论都必须解释它们）
  1. 不同期限的利率随时间同向变动（co-movement）：短端涨时，长端通常也涨。
  2. 短端利率低时曲线多呈陡峭向上；短端利率高时曲线多平坦甚至倒挂：期限价差与短端水平负相关。
  3. 长期利率平均高于短期利率：平均而言曲线向上倾斜。

三个理论各自能解释其中一部分：UET 解释 1 与倒挂；流动性溢价理论解释 1、2、3（最完整）；市场分割理论解释特定区段的异常。

2. 三大期限结构理论 Three Theories

Chapter 2.2 · slides 3–35Practice Q8, Q9(c)

2.1 无偏预期理论 Unbiased Expectations Theory (UET)

▸ Definition

Unbiased expectations theory: long-term rates are the geometric average of current and expected future short-term rates; investors view different maturities as perfect substitutes, so no maturity premium exists.

UET \[ (1+z_n)^n = (1+i_1)(1+i_2^e)\cdots(1+i_n^e) \]
\(z_n\) = \(n\) 年期即期利率（spot rate）；\(i_t^e\) = 预期的第 \(t\) 年一年期利率。注意用几何平均（相乘后开方），不是算术平均。
▸ UET 解释不了什么？

如果不同期限是完全替代品且没有期限溢价，那么预期的短期利率上升与下降应当同样常见，曲线平均应该是平的。但现实中曲线平均向上倾斜（事实 3）——这正是 UET 的短板，也是流动性溢价理论存在的理由。

2.2 流动性溢价理论 Liquidity Premium Theory

▸ Definition

Liquidity premium theory: long-term rates equal the geometric average of expected short rates plus a positive, maturity-specific liquidity (term) premium. Investors prefer short maturities, so borrowers must pay a premium to issue long.

流动性溢价 \[ z_n = \underbrace{\left[\prod_{t=1}^{n}(1+i_t^e)\right]^{1/n}-1}_{\text{UET 部分}} + LRP_n,\qquad LRP_n \text{ 随 } n \text{ 递增} \]
▸ 必考结论：向上倾斜 ≠ 预期短期利率上升

在流动性溢价理论下，即使预期未来短期利率完全不变甚至下降，只要期限溢价为正且随期限递增，长端收益率仍会高于短端，曲线仍会向上倾斜。
答题句式：An increasing liquidity premium can raise longer yields even when expected short-term rates are constant or declining. The observed slope reflects both expectations and premiums.

2.3 市场分割理论 Market Segmentation Theory

▸ Definition

Market segmentation theory: each maturity segment is a separate market whose yield is determined by its own supply and demand; investors have strong maturity preferences and do not substitute across maturities.
在该理论下，养老金等机构对长期债的需求上升 → 长端价格上升、收益率下降 → 在短端不变时价差收窄，曲线变平甚至倒挂。

EXAMPLE 1 Spot rates under UET and with liquidity premiums
EN

The current one-year spot rate is 3%. Expected one-year rates for Years 2 and 3 are 4% and 5%, respectively. All rates use annual compounding.
(a) Under unbiased expectations theory, calculate the two-year and three-year spot rates using geometric compounding.
(b) Sketch the resulting yield curve for maturities of one, two and three years. Explain its slope in terms of expected short-term rates.
(c) Now add maturity-specific liquidity premiums of 0.20 percentage points to the two-year rate and 0.40 percentage points to the three-year rate calculated in part (a). Calculate and compare the new rates.
(d) Explain why an upward-sloping yield curve does not necessarily imply rising expected short-term rates under liquidity premium theory.

中文分析

(a) UET 的核心是「两条投资路径的复利结果必须相同」：持有两年期零息债，等于连续持有两个一年期零息债。因此 先相乘，再开方。
(c) 溢价是在 UET 结果之上直接加（题目给的是百分点），并且要加到未四舍五入的数值上——这点很重要，先舍入再相加会累积误差。

公式 \[ (1+z_2)^2 = (1.03)(1.04) \qquad (1+z_3)^3 = (1.03)(1.04)(1.05) \]
解题步骤
  1. 两年期：\((1+z_2)^2=1.03\times1.04=1.0712\) → \(z_2=\sqrt{1.0712}-1=3.4988\%\approx\mathbf{3.50\%}\)。
  2. 三年期：\((1+z_3)^3=1.03\times1.04\times1.05=1.12476\) → \(z_3=1.12476^{1/3}-1=3.9968\%\approx\mathbf{4.00\%}\)。
  3. (b) 三个点：(1Y, 3.00%)、（2Y, 3.50%）、(3Y, 4.00%)，连成向上倾斜的曲线。
  4. (b) 斜率解释：长端包含了后续年份更高的预期短期利率（第 2 年 4%、第 3 年 5%）。
  5. (c) 加溢价：\(z_2=3.4988\%+0.20\%=\mathbf{3.70\%}\)；\(z_3=3.9968\%+0.40\%=\mathbf{4.40\%}\)；1 年期仍为 3.00%。
  6. (c) 比较：加入溢价后曲线更陡（价差从 1.00 pp 扩大到 1.40 pp）。
  7. (d) 因为期限溢价为正且随期限递增，即使预期短期利率不变甚至下降，长端仍可能更高 → 向上倾斜不能推出「预期短期利率上升」。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) \((1+z_2)^2=1.03\times1.04\), so \(z_2=\mathbf{3.4988\%}\approx3.50\%\). Similarly \(z_3=(1.03\times1.04\times1.05)^{1/3}-1=\mathbf{3.9968\%}\approx4.00\%\).
(b) Points (1Y, 3.00%), (2Y, 3.50%), (3Y, 4.00%) joined by an upward-sloping curve; longer rates incorporate the higher expected short rates in later years.
(c) Add the premiums to the unrounded rates: two-year = 3.4988% + 0.20% = 3.70%; three-year = 3.9968% + 0.40% = 4.40%; the one-year rate stays 3.00%. The curve is steeper.
(d) An increasing liquidity premium can raise longer yields even when expected short-term rates are constant or declining; the observed slope reflects both expectations and premiums.

中文答案

(a) 两年期 3.50%，三年期 4.00%（未舍入为 3.4988% 与 3.9968%）。
(b) 曲线向上倾斜，因为长端包含了更高的预期短期利率。
(c) 加溢价后：两年 3.70%、三年 4.40%，曲线更陡。
(d) 正的期限溢价可以让长端更高，即使预期短期利率不变或下降。
易错点：溢价要加在未舍入的数值上；且必须用几何平均，不能用算术平均。

3. 远期利率 Forward Rates

Chapter 2.2 · slides 24–35Practice Q9(a)(b)
▸ Definition

Forward rate（远期利率）: the interest rate for a future period that is implied today by the current term structure. It is the rate that makes two investment strategies deliver the same compounded payoff.

远期利率恒等式 \[ (1+z_2)^2 = (1+z_1)(1+f_{1,2}) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad f_{1,2}=\frac{(1+z_2)^2}{1+z_1}-1 \]
\(z_1\)、\(z_2\) = 1 年与 2 年即期利率；\(f_{1,2}\) = 从第 1 年末到第 2 年末的隐含一年期远期利率。这是恒等式，任何理论下都成立。
▸ 易错点：把远期利率当成「预测」需要额外假设

上面的公式只是一个无套利恒等式——它保证两条路径的复利结果相同，但它不说明 \(f_{1,2}\) 等于市场对未来即期利率的预期。要把 \(f_{1,2}\) 读作预期未来即期利率，必须额外假设不存在期限/流动性溢价，也就是 UET 成立。
答题句式：The expectations-theory assumption is needed: no term/liquidity premium separates the implied forward rate from the expected future short rate. The compounding identity alone does not imply an unbiased forecast.

EXAMPLE 2 Implied forward rate and its interpretation
EN

The one-year spot rate is 4.0% and the two-year spot rate is 4.5%, with annual compounding.
(a) Calculate the implied one-year forward rate for the period from the end of Year 1 to the end of Year 2. Show the compound-return relationship.
(b) Explain the extra assumption needed to interpret that forward rate as the expected future one-year spot rate.

中文分析

(a) 两年期路径的总复利是 \((1.045)^2\)；一年期 + 远期的路径是 \((1.04)(1+f)\)。令两者相等解出 \(f\)。
(b) 恒等式本身不含预测含义；需要 UET 假设才能把 \(f\) 读成预期。

公式 \[ (1.045)^2 = (1.04)(1+f_{1,2}) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad f_{1,2}=\frac{1.045^2}{1.04}-1 \]
解题步骤
  1. 两年路径总复利：\((1.045)^2=1.092025\)。
  2. 拆出第一年：\(\dfrac{1.092025}{1.04}=1.050024\)。
  3. 减 1：\(f_{1,2}=5.0024\%\approx\mathbf{5.00\%}\)。
  4. 校验：\((1.04)(1.050024)=1.092025\) ✓ 与两年路径一致。
  5. (b) 额外假设：不存在期限/流动性溢价（即 UET 成立），远期利率才等于预期未来即期利率。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) \((1.045)^2=(1.04)(1+f_{1,2})\), so \(f_{1,2}=1.045^2/1.04-1=5.0024\%\approx\mathbf{5.00\%}\).
(b) The expectations-theory assumption is needed: there is no relevant term/liquidity premium separating the implied forward rate from the expected future short rate. The compounding identity alone does not imply an unbiased forecast.

中文答案

(a) 隐含远期利率 5.00%（精确 5.0024%）。
(b) 需要假设没有期限/流动性溢价（UET 成立），才能把它读作预期未来即期利率。恒等式本身不构成预测。

4. 曲线移动的分类 Curve Movements

Chapter 2.2 · slides 39–45, 49–51Practice Q9(c)(d)(e)
▸ Definition

Bull / bear（牛/熊）: a bull movement is a broad decline in yields (bond prices rise); a bear movement is a broad rise in yields. Steepening / flattening（陡/平）: the curve steepens when the long-short spread widens and flattens when it narrows.

类型收益率水平期限价差典型成因
牛陡 Bull steepening下行 ↓扩大 ↑央行降息，短端降更多
牛平 Bull flattening下行 ↓收窄 ↓长端降更多（避险/预期恶化）
熊陡 Bear steepening上行 ↑扩大 ↑长端升更多（通胀预期上行）
熊平 Bear flattening上行 ↑收窄 ↓央行加息，短端升更多
▸ 倒挂曲线不是衰退的精确预测

倒挂通常反映未来政策宽松的预期或经济走弱，但收益率同时受期限溢价与特定期限的供需（市场分割）影响。因此倒挂不能给出衰退的确切时点、持续时间或严重程度。
答题句式：An inversion can reflect expectations of future easing or weakness, but premiums and maturity-specific demand also affect yields; it does not identify an exact recession date, duration or severity.

EXAMPLE 3 Segmentation, and classifying a curve move
EN

(a) Under market segmentation theory, suppose pension funds increase their demand for long-term bonds while short-term market conditions stay unchanged. Explain the effect on long-term bond prices, yields, and the yield-curve slope.
(b) A different yield curve moves from 4% at two years and 5% at ten years to 3% and 4.5%, respectively. Identify the movement as bull or bear, and steepening or flattening. Explain your classification.
(c) Why should an inverted yield curve not be treated as a precise forecast of the timing or severity of a recession?

中文分析

(a) 需求上升 → 价格上升 → 收益率下降（价格与收益率反向）；短端不变 → 价差收窄 → 变平或倒挂。
(b) 两个期限的收益率都下降 → 牛；价差从 1.0 pp 扩大到 1.5 pp → 陡；合起来是牛陡。
(c) 见上方要点框。

解题步骤
  1. (a) 养老金需求↑ → 长期债券价格上升。
  2. (a) 价格↑ → 长期收益率下降。
  3. (a) 短端不变 → 价差（长−短）下降 → 曲线变平或更倒挂。
  4. (b) 水平：4%→3%（2Y）与 5%→4.5%（10Y），都在下降 → 牛（bull）。
  5. (b) 斜率：价差从 \(5\%-4\%=1.0\) pp 变为 \(4.5\%-3\%=1.5\) pp，扩大 → 陡（steepening）。
  6. (b) 结论：牛陡 bull steepening。
  7. (c) 倒挂同时受预期、溢价与期限供需影响 → 不能给出衰退的时点、时长与严重程度。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) Greater demand raises long-term bond prices and lowers their yields. With short-term yields unchanged, the long-minus-short spread falls, so the curve flattens or becomes more inverted. Maturity preferences let the effect concentrate in the long segment.
(b) Both yields fall → bull. The ten-year-minus-two-year spread rises from 1.0 to 1.5 pp → steepening. Hence bull steepening.
(c) An inversion can reflect expectations of future easing or weakness, but premiums and maturity-specific demand also affect yields; it does not identify an exact recession date, duration or severity.

中文答案

(a) 长端价格↑、收益率↓；短端不变 → 曲线变平或更倒挂。
(b) 牛陡（bull steepening）：两端利率都降（牛），价差从 1.0 pp 扩大到 1.5 pp（陡）。
(c) 倒挂受预期、溢价、期限供需共同影响，不能精确预测衰退的时点与严重程度。
判断口诀：两端都降 = 牛；价差扩大 = 陡。

EN Answer

(a) Yes — in every adjacent interval all four maturities move the same way (e.g. T1→T3 all up; T6→T8 all up; T8→T10 all down).
(b) Spreads: 1.20, 1.20, 1.20, 1.12, 0.92, 0.35, −0.65, −1.08, −0.42, 0.65 → upward, upward, upward, upward, upward, upward, inverted, inverted, inverted, upward.
(c) Low group avg = 1.20 pp; medium = 0.76 pp; high = −0.72 pp. Low short rates go with steep curves; high short rates with flat/inverted curves.
(d) \(\bar y_1=3.385\%\), \(\bar y_{10}=3.834\%\), difference +0.449 pp: long rates exceed short rates on average.
(e) Co-movement; slope depends inversely on the short-rate level; long rates exceed short rates on average.

中文答案

①四个期限在每个相邻区间都同向变动；②价差 T1–T6 为正（向上）、T7–T9 为负（倒挂）、T10 为正；③低/中/高短端组的平均价差为 +1.20 / +0.76 / −0.72 pp，呈明显的单调递减；④\(\bar y_{10}-\bar y_1=+0.449\) pp。
三条规律分别对应课件的三个经验事实，答题时要明确点出这个对应关系。

Q Three-year forward rate chain 拓展
EN

Spot rates are \(z_1=2.0\%\), \(z_2=3.0\%\), \(z_3=3.6\%\) (annual compounding).
(a) Compute the implied one-year forward rates \(f_{1,2}\) and \(f_{2,3}\).
(b) If you believed UET, what would these forward rates tell you about expected future one-year rates?

查看解答 / Answer
中文分析

两年期与三年期分别用「两年路径 = 一年 + 远期」与「三年路径 = 两年 + 远期」的恒等式。第二个远期 \(f_{2,3}\) 要用两年期的总复利 \((1+z_3)^3\) 除以 \((1+z_2)^2\)。

  1. \(f_{1,2}=\dfrac{(1.03)^2}{1.02}-1=\dfrac{1.0609}{1.02}-1=4.0098\%\approx\mathbf{4.01\%}\)。
  2. \(f_{2,3}=\dfrac{(1.036)^3}{(1.03)^2}-1=\dfrac{1.111934}{1.0609}-1=4.8114\%\approx\mathbf{4.81\%}\)。
  3. 校验：\((1.02)(1.040098)(1.048114)=1.111934=(1.036)^3\) ✓。
  4. (b) 若 UET 成立，则 \(f_{1,2}\) 与 \(f_{2,3}\) 分别是市场预期的第 2 年与第 3 年一年期即期利率，即预期短端逐步上行（2% → 4.01% → 4.81%）。
EN Answer

(a) \(f_{1,2}=1.03^2/1.02-1=\mathbf{4.01\%}\); \(f_{2,3}=1.036^3/1.03^2-1=\mathbf{4.81\%}\). (b) Under UET these would be the market's expected one-year rates in Years 2 and 3, implying a rising short-rate path — though under liquidity premium theory part of the rise would instead be premium.

中文答案

(a) \(f_{1,2}=\)4.01%，\(f_{2,3}=\)4.81%。(b) 在 UET 下它们就是预期的第 2、3 年一年期利率；但在流动性溢价理论下，其中一部分应归因为期限溢价而非预期加息。

Q Forward rates and a risk-free arbitrage 拓展
EN

Observed spot rates: r1 = 4.00%, r2 = 5.00%, r3 = 5.60%.
(a) Compute the one-year forward rate one year from now, f(1,1), and the one-year forward rate two years from now, f(2,1).
(b) A bank quotes a forward borrowing rate of 5.80% for the year beginning in one year. Is this too high or too low relative to the fair rate?
(c) Describe the arbitrage and compute the risk-free profit per ¥100 of notional, stating when it is received.

查看解答 / Answer
中文分析

远期利率由即期利率唯一确定（无套利）：\((1+r_n)^n=(1+r_{n-1})^{n-1}(1+f)\)。若报价偏离这个值，就可以用「借长贷短」复制出远期头寸来套取差价。

  1. (a) \(f(1,1)=\dfrac{(1.05)^{2}}{1.04}-1=\dfrac{1.102500}{1.04}-1=\) 6.010%。
  2. (a) \(f(2,1)=\dfrac{(1.056)^{3}}{(1.05)^{2}}-1=\dfrac{1.177584}{1.102500}-1=\) 6.810%。
  3. (b) 报价 5.80% 低于公允值 6.010% → 作为借入方这个报价便宜。
  4. (c) 操作：按 5.80% 远期借入（t=1 收到 ¥100，t=2 偿还 ¥105.80）；同时合成一笔远期贷出——t=0 借入 2 年期 ¥96.15（=100/1.04）并贷出 1 年期同额，t=1 付出 ¥100，t=2 收到 ¥106.01。
  5. (c) t=2 净现金流 = 106.0096 − 105.80 = ¥0.21（每 ¥100 名义本金）。
  6. (c) 现值 = 0.2096 ÷ (1.05)2 = 0.2096 ÷ 1.1025 = ¥0.19。
EN Answer

(a) f(1,1) = 1.052/1.04 − 1 = 6.010%; f(2,1) = 1.0563/1.052 − 1 = 6.810%. (b) The quoted 5.80% is below the fair 6.010%, so it is cheap to borrow forward. (c) Borrow forward at 5.80% and create a synthetic forward loan at 6.010% by borrowing two-year and lending one-year. Net at t=2 = ¥0.21 per ¥100 notional, worth about ¥0.19 today.

中文答案

(a) f(1,1) = 6.010%，f(2,1) = 6.810%。(b) 报价 5.80% 低于公允值，远期借入更便宜。(c) 远期借入 5.80% + 合成远期贷出 6.010%，t=2 每 ¥100 净赚 ¥0.21，现值约 ¥0.19。

Q Pure expectations versus the liquidity premium theory 拓展
EN

The current 1-year rate is 3.00%. Expected 1-year rates are 4.00% in year 2, 5.00% in year 3 and 4.50% in year 4.
(a) Under the pure expectations theory, compute the 2-, 3- and 4-year spot rates and describe the shape of the curve.
(b) Now assume a liquidity (maturity) premium of 0.25% per extra year of maturity, i.e. MRPn = 0.25% × (n − 1). Recompute the curve and describe the new shape.
(c) Explain why the liquidity premium theory can produce an upward-sloping curve even when future short rates are expected to be flat.

查看解答 / Answer
中文分析

纯预期假说下，长端即期利率是当期与预期短期利率的几何平均：\((1+r_n)^n=\prod_{i=1}^{n}(1+r_i^{e})\)。加入流动性溢价只需在每一档额外加上 MRPn。

  1. (a) \(r_2=\left[(1.03)(1.04)\right]^{1/2}-1=(1.0712)^{1/2}-1=\) 3.499%。
  2. (a) \(r_3=\left[(1.03)(1.04)(1.05)\right]^{1/3}-1=(1.12476)^{1/3}-1=\) 3.997%。
  3. (a) \(r_4=\left[(1.03)(1.04)(1.05)(1.045)\right]^{1/4}-1=(1.175374)^{1/4}-1=\) 4.122%。
  4. (a) 形状：3.00 → 3.50 → 4.00 → 4.12，向上倾斜（预期短端先升后略降）。
  5. (b) r1 = 3.000 + 0 = 3.000%；r2 = 3.499 + 0.25 = 3.749%。
  6. (b) r3 = 3.997 + 0.50 = 4.497%；r4 = 4.122 + 0.75 = 4.872% → 更陡峭地向上倾斜。
  7. (c) 若预期短端持平，纯预期假说给出水平曲线；加上随期限递增的 MRP 后，长端 = 短端 + MRPn → 曲线必然向上倾斜。这正是流动性溢价理论解释「收益率曲线通常向上倾斜」的方式。
EN Answer

(a) r2 = 3.499%, r3 = 3.997%, r4 = 4.122% — upward sloping. (b) 3.000%, 3.749%, 4.497%, 4.872% — more steeply upward sloping. (c) With flat expected short rates the pure expectations theory gives a flat curve; the rising maturity premium makes the long end exceed the short end, so the curve slopes up — which is why the curve is upward sloping on average.

中文答案

(a) 3.499% / 3.997% / 4.122%，向上倾斜。(b) 3.000% / 3.749% / 4.497% / 4.872%，更陡峭。(c) 预期持平时纯预期假说给出水平曲线，但递增的期限溢价使长端更高 → 向上倾斜。
