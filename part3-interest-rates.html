1. 货币时间价值复习 Time Value of Money

Chapter 2.0 Review · slides 3–16Practice Q5
▸ Definition

Time value of money (TVM): a unit of currency received today is worth more than the same unit received in the future, because it can be invested to earn a return. Discounting converts future cash flows into present value; compounding converts present value into future value.

单笔现金流 \[ FV = PV(1+r)^n \qquad PV=\frac{FV}{(1+r)^n} \]
\(PV\) = 现值 present value；\(FV\) = 终值 future value；\(r\) = 每期利率；\(n\) = 期数。
年金 \[ PV_{\text{annuity}} = C\cdot\frac{1-(1+r)^{-n}}{r} \qquad FV_{\text{annuity}} = C\cdot\frac{(1+r)^n-1}{r} \]
\(C\) = 每期等额现金流。课件练习默认期末支付（ordinary annuity，payment occurs at period-end），这也是全站的默认假设。
▸ 解题定式
  1. 画时间轴，标出每期的现金流与折现率；
  2. 确认是单笔还是等额系列（年金），以及是否期末支付；
  3. 选公式代入，保留中间精度，最后再四舍五入；
  4. 比较方案时，必须折算到同一时点再比（要么都折到今天，要么都算到同一未来日）。
EXAMPLE 1 Present value of an instalment plan
EN

A student can buy a computer for $9,500 today or make three payments of $3,500 at the end of Years 1, 2 and 3. The appropriate annual discount rate is 5%.
(a) Calculate the present value of the instalment plan and identify the cheaper payment option in present-value terms.
(b) Calculate the future value at the end of Year 3 of $9,500 invested today at 5% per year.
(c) The student instead saves $3,000 at the end of each of the next three years. Calculate the accumulated value immediately after the third deposit.
(d) Explain how a higher discount rate affects the present value of the instalments. Distinguish a change in present value from a change in the contractual payment amounts.

中文分析

(a) 分期方案是一个三年期期末年金，把它折到现在与 $9,500 直接比较即可。注意：分期总额 $10,500 大于 $9,500，但因为钱是未来付的，现值可能更小——这正是本题的考点。
(c) 第三笔存款在第三年末才存入，因此不计息；这是年金终值题最常见的坑。

公式 \[ PV = \frac{3{,}500}{1.05}+\frac{3{,}500}{1.05^2}+\frac{3{,}500}{1.05^3} \] \[ FV_{\text{annuity}} = 3{,}000\,(1.05^2+1.05+1) \]
解题步骤
  1. (a) 逐项折现：\(\dfrac{3{,}500}{1.05}=3{,}333.33\)；\(\dfrac{3{,}500}{1.05^2}=3{,}174.60\)；\(\dfrac{3{,}500}{1.05^3}=3{,}023.43\)。
  2. (a) 求和：\(PV=3{,}333.33+3{,}174.60+3{,}023.43=\mathbf{\$9{,}531.37}\)。
  3. (a) 比较：\(9{,}531.37-9{,}500.00=\$31.37\)，今天一次性付 $9,500 更便宜 \$31.37。
  4. (b) 单笔终值：\(FV=9{,}500\times1.05^3=\mathbf{\$10{,}997.44}\)。
  5. (c) 年金终值：第一笔存 2 年、第二笔存 1 年、第三笔存 0 年 → \(3{,}000(1.05^2+1.05+1)=3{,}000\times3.1525=\mathbf{\$9{,}457.50}\)。
  6. (d) 折现率越高 → 未来现金流的现值越低；但三笔 $3,500 的合同金额不变——变的是「今天值多少钱」，不是「要付多少」。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) \(PV=3{,}500/1.05+3{,}500/1.05^2+3{,}500/1.05^3=\mathbf{\$9{,}531.37}\). Paying \$9,500 today is cheaper by \$31.37 in present-value terms.
(b) \(FV=9{,}500\times1.05^3=\mathbf{\$10{,}997.44}\).
(c) The first deposit earns two years of interest, the second one year and the final deposit none: \(FV=3{,}000(1.05^2+1.05+1)=\mathbf{\$9{,}457.50}\).
(d) A higher discount rate lowers the present value of the future instalments. It changes their value today, not the three contractual payments of \$3,500 each.

中文答案

(a) 分期方案现值 \$9,531.37，一次性付 \$9,500 更便宜（省 \$31.37）。
(b) 终值 \$10,997.44；(c) 三年后累计 \$9,457.50。
(d) 折现率上升 → 分期现值下降，但合同付款额 \$3,500 × 3 不变。
两个易错点：①(c) 中最后一笔存款不计息；②(a) 的比较必须在同一时点进行。

▸ 记忆技巧：年金终值为什么是 \((1+r)^{n-1}\) 到 \((1+r)^0\)？

第 1 笔存款在第 1 年末存入，到第 \(n\) 年末还能再存 \(n-1\) 年；最后一笔在第 \(n\) 年末存入，立刻就到估值时点，因此指数为 0，系数为 1。写成通式就是 \(C\big[(1+r)^{n-1}+\cdots+(1+r)+1\big]\)。

2. 可贷资金理论 Loanable Funds Theory

Chapter 2.1 Determinants · slides 5–14Practice Q6
▸ Definition

Loanable funds theory treats the interest rate as the price of credit, determined by the supply of funds (mainly household saving) and the demand for funds (mainly business investment and government borrowing). The equilibrium interest rate equates the two.
可贷资金理论把利率视为信用的价格：由资金供给（主要是家庭储蓄）与资金需求（主要是企业投资）共同决定，均衡时两者相等。

冲击哪条曲线移动方向均衡利率均衡数量
家庭在每个利率下都存得更多供给 S右移 ↑下降上升
企业更乐观、想借更多需求 D右移 ↑上升上升
政府赤字扩大需求 D右移 ↑上升上升
家庭储蓄与企业借款需求同时增加S 与 D 都右移—方向不确定上升（确定）
▸ 必考辨析：沿曲线移动 vs 曲线平移
  • 沿曲线移动（movement along）：利率本身变化导致供给量/需求量变化。例如利率上升使家庭愿意多存 → 沿着既定的供给曲线向上移动。
  • 曲线平移（shift）：利率以外的因素变化，使得在每一个利率水平上的供给量或需求量都变了。例如偏好变化、收入变化、投资机会变化。

答题句式：A change in the interest rate itself causes movement along a given supply curve; an increase in saving at each possible rate shifts the whole supply curve outward.

▸ 供需同增时为什么利率方向不确定？

供给右移压低利率，需求右移推高利率，两者方向相反，最终取决于哪条曲线移动得更多。但均衡数量一定上升（两条曲线都往「量更大」的方向推）。这是课件明确要求的结论：the rate effect is ambiguous; the equilibrium quantity increases.

EXAMPLE 2 Shifting the loanable-funds curves
EN

(a) Draw a loanable-funds diagram and label the supply curve, demand curve, equilibrium interest rate and equilibrium quantity.
(b) Households decide to save more at every interest rate. Identify the curve that shifts and explain the effects on the equilibrium rate and quantity.
(c) Firms become more optimistic about investment projects and wish to borrow more. Repeat the analysis.
(d) Explain the difference between a movement along the supply curve caused by a higher interest rate and an outward shift of that curve.
(e) If household saving and business borrowing demand both increase, can the direction of the interest-rate change be determined without more information? Explain.

中文分析

画图题的评分点通常是「坐标轴 + 两条曲线斜率 + 交点标注」这三件事。之后每一问都按「哪条曲线 → 往哪移 → 利率如何 → 数量如何」的固定顺序作答，就不会漏点。

解题步骤
  1. (a) 纵轴 = 利率 \(r\)，横轴 = 可贷资金数量 \(Q\)；供给向上倾斜、需求向下倾斜；交点 = \((r^*, Q^*)\)。
  2. (b) 储蓄在每个利率下都增加 → 供给曲线右移；需求不变 → 均衡利率下降、均衡数量上升。
  3. (c) 企业借款需求增加 → 需求曲线右移；供给不变 → 均衡利率上升、均衡数量上升。
  4. (d) 利率上升本身 → 沿供给曲线向上移动；而「每个利率下都多存」→ 整条供给曲线右移。
  5. (e) 供给右移压低利率、需求右移推高利率 → 方向不能确定，取决于相对移动幅度；但均衡数量一定增加。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) Vertical axis = interest rate, horizontal axis = quantity of loanable funds; supply slopes up, demand slopes down; their intersection gives the equilibrium rate and quantity.
(b) Greater saving at every rate shifts supply right: the equilibrium interest rate falls and the quantity rises.
(c) Greater desired borrowing shifts demand right: both the equilibrium rate and quantity rise.
(d) A change in the interest rate itself causes movement along a given supply curve; an increase in saving at each possible rate shifts the whole curve outward.
(e) No — higher supply pushes the rate down while higher demand pushes it up; their relative shifts determine the result. In the standard model the equilibrium quantity increases.

中文答案

(a) 纵轴利率、横轴资金量，供给向上、需求向下，交点即均衡。(b) 储蓄增加 → 供给右移 → 利率降、数量升。(c) 投资需求增加 → 需求右移 → 利率升、数量升。(d) 利率变动 = 沿曲线移动；每个利率下都多存 = 曲线平移。(e) 利率方向不确定（取决于相对幅度），但数量一定增加。

3. 费雪效应与通胀 Fisher Effect

Chapter 2.1 Determinants · slides 16–29Practice Q7(a)
▸ Definition

Fisher effect: the nominal interest rate adjusts one-for-one with expected inflation, leaving the real interest rate (the return in terms of purchasing power) unchanged.

费雪关系 \[ \text{近似：}\quad r_{nom} \approx r_{real} + E(\pi) \] \[ \text{精确：}\quad (1+r_{nom}) = (1+r_{real})\,(1+E(\pi)) \]
\(r_{nom}\) = 名义利率 nominal rate；\(r_{real}\) = 实际利率 real rate；\(E(\pi)\) = 预期通胀率 expected inflation。课件与练习默认使用近似式，除非题目另有说明。
▸ 为什么两个式子都成立？

精确式来自「名义回报 = 实际回报 × 购买力变化」的相乘关系。展开后得到 \(r_{nom}=r_{real}+E(\pi)+r_{real}E(\pi)\)，最后一项是两个百分数的乘积，数值很小，因此近似式丢掉的就是这个交叉项。利率水平越低，近似越准。

EXAMPLE 3 Applying the Fisher relation
EN

A one-year nominal risk-free rate is 4.0% and expected inflation is 2.5%.
(a) Calculate the approximate real risk-free rate. If expected inflation rises to 3.5% while the real rate stays unchanged, calculate the new nominal risk-free rate.
(b) Recalculate part (a) using the exact (multiplicative) Fisher relationship, and compare the two answers.

中文分析

(a) 近似式直接相减即可：实际利率 ≈ 名义 − 预期通胀。然后固定实际利率不变，把新的通胀预期代回去，得到新的名义利率——通胀预期上升 1 个百分点，名义利率也上升 1 个百分点，这正是费雪效应的含义。
(b) 精确式要先除后减，顺序不能反。

公式 \[ r_{real}\approx r_{nom}-E(\pi) \qquad r_{real}=\frac{1+r_{nom}}{1+E(\pi)}-1 \]
解题步骤
  1. (a) 近似实际利率 \(=4.0\%-2.5\%=\mathbf{1.50\%}\)。
  2. (a) 通胀升至 3.5%，实际利率不变 → 新名义利率 \(=1.50\%+3.5\%=\mathbf{5.00\%}\)。
  3. (b) 精确实际利率 \(=\dfrac{1.04}{1.025}-1=1.4634\%\approx\mathbf{1.46\%}\)。
  4. (b) 新名义利率 \(=(1.014634)(1.035)-1=5.0146\%\approx\mathbf{5.01\%}\)。
  5. 比较：近似值 1.50% / 5.00%，精确值 1.46% / 5.01%，差异约 4 个基点——在低利率水平下近似误差很小。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) Approximate real risk-free rate = 4.0% − 2.5% = 1.50%. With that real rate unchanged, the new nominal rate = 1.50% + 3.5% = 5.00%.
(b) Exact: \(r_{real}=1.04/1.025-1=1.4634\%\); new nominal \(=(1.014634)(1.035)-1=\mathbf{5.0146\%}\). The approximation differs by only about 1.5 bp and 1.5 bp respectively at these low rates.

中文答案

(a) 近似实际利率 1.50%；通胀预期升至 3.5% 后名义利率 5.00%。
(b) 精确式：实际利率 1.4634%，新名义利率 5.0146%。两者差异很小（约 1–2 个基点）。
核心结论：实际利率不变时，预期通胀上升多少，名义利率就上升多少。

4. 利率的加总分解 Rate Decomposition

Chapter 2.1 Determinants · slides 16–29Practice Q7(b)(c)(d)
加总分解式 \[ r = r_{real} + IP + DRP + LRP + MRP \]
\(r_{real}\) 实际利率 real risk-free rate：剔除通胀后的真实资金成本。
\(IP\) 通胀溢价 inflation premium：补偿购买力损失。
\(DRP\) 违约风险溢价 default risk premium：补偿借款人可能不按约偿付的风险。
\(LRP\) 流动性溢价 liquidity risk premium：补偿难以按可预期价格快速变现的成本。
\(MRP\) 期限风险溢价 maturity risk premium：补偿长期利率不确定性（与 Part 4 的期限溢价相关）。
EXAMPLE 4 Required yield with default and liquidity premiums
EN

A one-year nominal risk-free rate is 4.0% and expected inflation is 2.5%.
(a) A corporate security has a default risk premium of 1.2% and a liquidity risk premium of 0.4%. Using the original 4.0% benchmark and assuming no other premiums, calculate its required yield.
(b) The corporate yield subsequently rises by 0.6 percentage points while the matched Treasury yield stays unchanged. Explain why this need not mean that default risk alone has increased.
(c) Explain why investors normally demand a higher yield on a less liquid security, other things equal. Distinguish a high promised yield from a guaranteed high realized return.

中文分析

(a) 4.0% 已经是名义无风险利率（已包含 \(r_{real}\) 与 \(IP\)），因此不要再重复加通胀——直接在它上面叠加 DRP 与 LRP 即可。这是最容易多算一步的地方。
(b) 价差（spread）扩大了 60 bp，但价差是多个溢价的合计数，任何一项上升都能造成同样结果，因此不能反推出「只有违约风险上升」。
(c) 承诺收益率（promised yield）是按合同应付的；实现收益率（realized return）要看实际收到多少——违约、折价抛售都会让实现收益低于承诺值。

公式 \[ \text{Spread} = r_{corporate} - r_{Treasury} = DRP + LRP + \text{其他溢价} \]
解题步骤
  1. (a) 基准：名义无风险利率 = 4.0%（已含实际利率与通胀溢价）。
  2. (a) 加总：\(4.0\%+1.2\%+0.4\%=\mathbf{5.60\%}\)。
  3. (b) 国库券收益率不变 → 企业债收益率上升 0.6 pp 意味着价差扩大 60 bp。
  4. (b) 解释：价差 = DRP + LRP + 其他；上升可能来自违约风险、流动性恶化，或两者兼有 → 不能单独归因于违约风险。
  5. (c) 低流动性证券难以及时按可预期价格卖出，投资者要求补偿 → 收益率更高。
  6. (c) 区分：高承诺收益率可能只是对预期违约损失或流动性成本的补偿，实现收益率可能更低甚至为负。
查看解答 / Answer
EN Answer

(a) Required yield = 4.0% + 1.2% + 0.4% = 5.60%.
(b) The spread over the matched Treasury rises by 0.6 pp (60 bp). The increase may reflect greater default risk, reduced liquidity, or both; a yield spread is not a pure default-risk measure unless other premiums are controlled for.
(c) An illiquid security may be hard to sell promptly at a predictable price, so investors demand compensation. A higher promised yield may partly compensate for expected default losses or other risks; the realized return can be lower, including negative.

中文答案

(a) 5.60%（4.0% 基准已含通胀，不要再叠加通胀）。
(b) 价差扩大 60 bp；原因可能是违约风险、流动性或两者皆有，不能只归因于违约风险。
(c) 流动性差 → 要求更高收益率作为补偿；但高承诺收益率 ≠ 高实现收益率，后者可能因违约或折价抛售而更低。

▸ 三个高频扣分点
  1. 重复计入通胀：若题目给的是名义无风险利率（如 4.0%），它已经包含 \(r_{real}+IP\)，不要再额外加一次 \(IP\)。
  2. 把价差当纯违约度量：价差 = DRP + LRP + 其他，任何一项变化都会改变价差。
  3. 混淆承诺与实现：承诺收益率是合同上的，实现收益率是实际拿到的。

  3. (c) 价差扩大 60 bp，可能来自违约风险、流动性或两者 → 不能只归因于违约。
  4. (d) 流动性差需补偿；承诺收益率 ≠ 实现收益率。
EN Answer

(a) 1.50%; new nominal 5.00%. (b) 5.60%. (c) The spread widened 60 bp; it may reflect default risk, liquidity, or both — a spread is not a pure default measure. (d) Illiquidity requires compensation; a high promised yield can still produce a low realized return.

中文答案

(a) 实际 1.50%，新名义 5.00%；(b) 5.60%；(c) 价差扩大 60 bp，原因可能是违约、流动性或两者；(d) 低流动性要求补偿，承诺收益 ≠ 实现收益。

Q Decomposing a yield spread 拓展
EN

A 5-year corporate bond yields 6.80% while a 5-year government bond yields 4.30%. The expected inflation rate over the next five years is 2.20%.
(a) What is the total spread? If DRP = 1.8%, what implied LRP is consistent with the data (assuming no other premiums)?
(b) If liquidity in the corporate market suddenly improves, what happens to the spread and to the price of the existing corporate bond, holding default risk constant?

查看解答 / Answer
中文分析

(a) 价差 = 企业债收益率 − 国债收益率。剩余部分在「无其他溢价」假设下全部归 LRP。(b) 流动性改善 → LRP 下降 → 价差收窄、企业债收益率下降 → 既有债券价格上升（价格与收益率反向）。

  1. 总价差 \(=6.80\%-4.30\%=2.50\%\)（即 250 bp）。
  2. 扣除 DRP：\(LRP=2.50\%-1.80\%=\mathbf{0.70\%}\)（70 bp）。
  3. 流动性改善 → \(LRP\) 下降 → 企业债要求收益率下降。
  4. 价格与收益率反向 → 存量企业债价格上升。
  5. 注意：这里假设违约风险不变，只让 LRP 变化；若违约风险同时变化，结论需重新分析。
EN Answer

(a) Spread = 6.80% − 4.30% = 2.50% (250 bp); implied LRP = 2.50% − 1.80% = 0.70%. (b) Better liquidity lowers LRP, narrows the spread and lowers the corporate yield, so the price of the existing corporate bond rises (holding default risk constant).

中文答案

(a) 价差 250 bp，隐含 LRP = 70 bp。(b) 流动性改善 → 价差收窄、收益率下降 → 存量债价格上涨。关键是把「溢价下降 → 收益率下降 → 价格上升」这条链完整写出来。

Q Fisher effect: expected versus unexpected inflation 拓展
EN

A 1-year Treasury bill offers a nominal yield of 5.50%. Expected inflation over the year is 3.00%.
(a) Compute the exact expected real interest rate and the approximate one. Why do they differ?
(b) Suppose actual inflation turns out to be 4.50%. Compute the realised real rate. Who gains and who loses?
(c) State the Fisher equation and explain what it says about how nominal yields respond to a rise in expected inflation.

查看解答 / Answer
中文分析

费雪方程的精确形式相乘、近似形式相加。\(1+r_{\text{nom}}=(1+r_{\text{real}})(1+\pi)\)。差异来自交叉项 \(r_{\text{real}}\times\pi\)，利率与通胀越高，近似误差越大。第二问考的是预期内 vs 预期外通胀的再分配效应。

  1. (a) 精确：\(r_{\text{real}}=\dfrac{1.0550}{1.0300}-1=0.024272=\) 2.427%。
  2. (a) 近似：5.50% − 3.00% = 2.50%；差 7.3 bp = 交叉项 \(r_{\text{real}}\pi\approx 0.0243\times0.03\)。
  3. (b) 实现实际利率 = \(\dfrac{1.0550}{1.0450}-1=0.009569=\) 0.957%。
  4. (b) 实际通胀高于预期 → 实际回报被侵蚀 → 借款人获益、贷款人受损（财富从债权人转移到债务人）。
  5. (c) 费雪方程：\(r_{\text{nom}}\approx r_{\text{real}}+\pi^{e}\)。预期通胀上升 1 pp，名义收益率大致上升 1 pp，而实际利率不变（费雪效应）。
EN Answer

(a) Exact: 1.0550/1.0300 − 1 = 2.427%; approximate: 5.50% − 3.00% = 2.50%. They differ by the cross-product term r_real × π. (b) Realised real rate = 1.0550/1.0450 − 1 = 0.957%. Because inflation came in above expectation, wealth is transferred from the lender to the borrower. (c) Fisher: r_nom ≈ r_real + πe; a 1 pp rise in expected inflation raises the nominal yield about 1 pp, leaving the real rate unchanged.

中文答案

(a) 精确实际利率 2.427%，近似 2.50%，差值为交叉项。(b) 实现实际利率 0.957%，通胀超预期使借款人受益、贷款人受损。(c) \(r_{\text{nom}}\approx r_{\text{real}}+\pi^{e}\)：预期通胀升 1 pp，名义收益率约升 1 pp。

Q Decomposing two yields into five premia 拓展
EN

Observed yields: 10-year Treasury 4.20%, 10-year corporate bond 6.45%. You estimate the real risk-free rate at 1.20%, expected inflation at 2.50%, the corporate default risk premium at 1.60%, and the corporate liquidity risk premium at 0.65%.
(a) Using \(r=r_{\text{real}}+\text{IP}+\text{DRP}+\text{LRP}+\text{MRP}\), solve for the maturity risk premium implied by the Treasury yield.
(b) Verify that the same decomposition reproduces the corporate yield.
(c) If expected inflation rises by 100 bp with everything else unchanged, what happens to both yields and to bond prices? Which bond's price falls by more, and why?

查看解答 / Answer
中文分析

分解公式是逐项相加。国债的 DRP 与 LRP 视为 0，因此它只含三项；公司债在此基础上再加 DRP 与 LRP。(c) 把「溢价上升 → 收益率上升 → 价格下跌」和 Malkiel 定理 2（期限越长越敏感）串起来。

  1. (a) 国债：4.20 = 1.20 + 2.50 + 0 + 0 + MRP → MRP = 4.20 − 3.70 = 0.50%（50 bp）。
  2. (b) 公司债：1.20 + 2.50 + 1.60 + 0.65 + 0.50 = 6.45% ✓ 与观测值一致。
  3. (b) 因此信用价差 = 6.45 − 4.20 = 225 bp = DRP 160 bp + LRP 65 bp。
  4. (c) IP 升 100 bp → 两个收益率各上升 100 bp，价格均下跌（价格与收益率反向）。
  5. (c) 因为两者期限同为 10 年、但公司债票息更高 → 公司债价格跌幅略小（Malkiel 定理 3：票息越高，价格对收益率越不敏感）。
EN Answer

(a) MRP = 4.20 − (1.20 + 2.50) = 0.50%. (b) 1.20 + 2.50 + 1.60 + 0.65 + 0.50 = 6.45%, matching the observed yield; the credit spread is 225 bp = 160 bp DRP + 65 bp LRP. (c) Both yields rise 100 bp and both prices fall. The corporate bond's price falls slightly less in percentage terms because its higher coupon makes it less price-sensitive (Malkiel's Theorem 3).

中文答案

(a) MRP = 0.50%。(b) 1.20+2.50+1.60+0.65+0.50 = 6.45%，与观测一致；信用价差 225 bp = DRP 160 + LRP 65。(c) 两者收益率各升 100 bp、价格均跌；公司债跌幅略小，因其票息更高、价格敏感度更低。

Q Quoted rate versus effective annual rate 拓展
EN

Bank A quotes 6.00% compounded monthly on a 1-year deposit. Bank B quotes 6.10% compounded annually.
(a) Compute the effective annual rate (EAR) of each.
(b) Which deposit is better, and by how many basis points?
(c) Explain in words why a lower quoted rate can beat a higher one, and state the general EAR formula.

查看解答 / Answer
中文分析

比较不同复利频率的报价时，必须换算到同一口径（EAR）。公式 \(\text{EAR}=\left(1+\frac{r_{\text{quoted}}}{m}\right)^{m}-1\)，m 为每年复利次数。复利频率越高，EAR 越高。

  1. (a) Bank A：\(\text{EAR}=\left(1+\dfrac{0.0600}{12}\right)^{12}-1=(1.005)^{12}-1\)。
  2. (a) \((1.005)^{12}=1.061678\) → EARA = 6.1678%。
  3. (a) Bank B：年复利，EARB = 6.1000%。
  4. (b) 6.1678% − 6.1000% = 0.0678% = 6.78 bp → Bank A 更优。
  5. (c) 原因：A 的报价虽低 10 bp，但每月复利产生的利滚利多出 16.78 bp，净胜 6.78 bp。
  6. (c) 结论：报价利率（APR）不可直接横向比较，必须看复利频率或统一换算为 EAR。
EN Answer

(a) EARA = (1 + 0.06/12)12 − 1 = 6.1678%; EARB = 6.1000%. (b) Bank A is better by 6.78 bp. (c) A's lower quoted rate is more than offset by monthly compounding: EAR = (1 + r_quoted/m)m − 1, and EAR rises with the compounding frequency m, so quoted rates with different m cannot be compared directly.

中文答案

(a) EARA = 6.1678%，EARB = 6.1000%。(b) A 更优，高 6.78 bp。(c) 复利频率更高带来额外利滚利；\(\text{EAR}=(1+r/m)^m-1\)，报价利率须换算后才可比较。
